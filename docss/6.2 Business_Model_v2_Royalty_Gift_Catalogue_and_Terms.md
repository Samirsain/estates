# Royalty Relationship Reward — Gift Catalogue & Programme Terms

**Business Model:** v2  
**Version:** 1.0 Draft for Final Approval  
**Date:** 9 October 2026  
**Effective Date:** No live activation until the dated Programme Version and Catalogue are approved  
**Timezone:** Asia/Kolkata

> These Terms govern the non-cash Royalty Relationship Reward and must be read with Business Model v2, the applicable Programme Version, Member Terms and transaction records.

## 1. Purpose

The Royalty Relationship Reward recognises one genuine long-term Customer relationship created through the Customer's qualifying first Member-sold purchase.

It is:
- non-cash;
- relationship-based;
- one-time per qualifying Customer opportunity;
- not passive income;
- not multi-level;
- not a monetary percentage.

## 2. No monetary Royalty

There are no Royalty percentage bands, positions, cycles or cash Royalty payouts.

> **One qualifying Royalty event = one Royalty Credit = one Catalogue Gift entitlement.**

## 3. Relationship creation

At the Primary Customer's earliest qualifying first purchase:

**Sold By Member:** selling Member becomes the Provisional Royalty Linked Member.

**Sold By Customer / Sold By 3% Club:** no Member Royalty relationship is created.

A later Member sale does not retroactively create the first-purchase relationship.

## 4. Primary Customer only

Only the Primary Customer establishes the Royalty relationship. Additional Customers do not each create separate Royalty relationships.

## 5. Relationship finalisation

The provisional relationship becomes final when the first qualifying purchase reaches:
- 100% verified Payment Received; or
- qualifying Approved Buyback.

## 6. Buyback minimum source payment

Approved Buyback can act as an alternative Royalty milestone only when the source Booking has already reached:

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

## 7. Cancellation before finalisation

If the first Booking is cancelled before both 100% Payment Received and a qualifying Approved Buyback:
- provisional relationship is removed from current entitlement;
- the opportunity is not consumed;
- a later genuine first qualifying purchase may establish the relationship.

History remains auditable.

## 8. One opportunity per Customer

Each qualifying Primary Customer creates one Royalty opportunity for life for the valid Royalty Linked Member.

## 9. Reward-triggering future purchase

The Royalty Linked Member may receive one Reward when that Customer later makes the first qualifying direct personal purchase through 3% Club, provided:
- the opportunity is unused;
- final Sold By is not another Member;
- the Programme is active;
- all other rules are satisfied.

## 10. Another Member closes the repeat purchase

If another Member is final Sold By:
- no Royalty Reward is generated on that sale;
- the opportunity remains unused.

## 11. Final Sold By controls Club-direct treatment

Under the approved Owner decision:

> **The final approved Sold By selection alone decides whether the repeat purchase is Club-direct.**

Background/informal involvement by the Royalty Linked Member does not automatically reclassify the sale.

Fraudulent data entry remains prohibited.

## 12. One Credit = one Gift

One qualifying event creates one Royalty Credit, which creates one Gift entitlement under the frozen Programme Version.

Credits do not combine into points unless a future approved Programme explicitly introduces that feature.

## 13. Programme Version freeze

The applicable Gift Programme Version freezes when:

> **the qualifying future Royalty Booking Request version is ultimately approved.**

Later Catalogue changes do not alter that entitlement.

## 14. Catalogue approval requirement

No live Royalty Gift promise may be made until the dated Programme Version and Catalogue are approved.

The live Catalogue must state:
- Reward Reference;
- category;
- product/specification;
- substitution rule;
- availability status;
- selection deadline;
- fulfilment timeline;
- delivery method;
- nominee rules.

- exact brand/model or defined equivalent;
- storage;
- colour availability rule;
- warranty;
- new/sealed requirement;
- substitution rule if discontinued or unavailable.

## 17. Gold standard

If gold is offered, the live Catalogue should state:
- exact weight;
- purity;
- hallmark/certification;
- coin/bar/jewellery form;
- packaging/certificate;
- substitution treatment.

Do not publicly promise “gold” without the approved specification.

## 18. Gift selection

After the Credit becomes fulfilment-ready:
- Member selects one available Gift from the frozen Catalogue Version;
- selection is recorded;
- selected Reward Reference is locked once ordering begins unless authorised substitution is required.

## 19. Selection deadline

The live Programme must define a selection deadline, for example `[__] days` after fulfilment-ready status.

Do not silently cancel an Earned entitlement unless the published Programme expressly provides for expiry.

## 20. Reward owner and recipient nominee

The Royalty Linked Member remains owner of the Credit.

Permitted recipient:
- immediate family; or
- one non-family nominee with MD approval.

Recipient identity must be recorded.

## 21. No automatic cash alternative

There is no automatic cash alternative for a Royalty Gift.

## 22. Buyback-triggered eligibility

Buyback itself does not earn Royalty.

Where Approved Buyback creates early eligibility:
- the Credit belongs to the already-established Royalty Linked Member;
- the Buyback arranger/Buying Commission beneficiary does not receive Royalty merely for arranging the acquisition.

## 23. Gift fulfilment after Buyback

Physical Gift fulfilment waits until either:
1. the underlying qualifying Customer purchase reaches 100% verified Payment Received; or
2. Buyback reaches Stable Buyback Completion.

## 24. Stable Buyback Completion

Requires:
1. Buyback/Acquisition formally Approved;
2. Payment Given = 100% verified;
3. no cancellation/unwind;
4. applicable acquisition completion/document-return conditions complete.

## 25. Buyback unwind

If Buyback-created eligibility later unwinds before fulfilment:
- eligibility reverses unless the underlying purchase independently reaches the normal milestone;
- no duplicate Royalty Credit is created.

## 26. Delivery finality

Once the Gift is delivered:

> **the Royalty opportunity and Credit used for that Gift remain consumed forever.**

Ordinary later transaction cancellation does not reopen the opportunity.

Fraud/recovery may still be pursued.

## 27. Recovery and deactivation

Recovery Outstanding or Member Deactivation:
- preserves relationship/entitlement history;
- holds physical fulfilment until the block is cleared.

## 28. Staff conflicts

Staff/defined close-relative Reward requires conflict disclosure and MD approval. Staff cannot approve their own entitlement.

## 29. Sold By correction after delivery

A correction after Gift delivery:
- does not automatically generate a second Gift;
- requires MD approval;
- any exceptional settlement is handled outside the reward engine.

## 30. Stock unavailability / discontinuation

The live Catalogue must define whether the Company may provide:
- same product from another authorised source;
- equivalent-or-better substitute;
- another Catalogue item;
- delayed fulfilment.

There is no automatic cash right unless expressly provided.

## 31. Variant availability

Colour/cosmetic variant may depend on stock unless specifically guaranteed. Approved core specification controls.

## 32. Warranty

Manufacturer/supplier warranty governs where applicable. 3% Club should provide appropriate purchase/warranty documentation where commercially and legally possible.

## 33. Delivery record

CRM should record/reference:
- selected Gift reference;
- selected date;
- recipient;
- ordered date;
- dispatch/fulfilment reference;
- delivered date;
- fulfilment status.

## 34. Damaged / wrong item

The live operational Terms should define a reporting period for:
- damaged item;
- wrong item/specification;
- missing certificate/accessory.

Supplier/manufacturer warranty applies after accepted delivery where relevant.

## 35. Fraud

No Reward is valid for fake Customers, duplicate identity abuse, false Sold By, sham Bookings, fabricated first purchases, collusion or material misrepresentation.

## 36. Tax / statutory treatment

Applicable tax, withholding and reporting treatment follows law and professional advice.

Do not advertise the Gift as tax-free or no-TDS unless professionally confirmed.

## 37. No guarantee of future Catalogue value

Future Programme Versions may change Gift choices/specifications/value. The frozen Programme Version controls the entitlement.

## 38. Programme Version control

Every live Programme must have:
- Programme Version;
- Catalogue Version;
- approval date;
- approver;
- effective date/time.

Material changes require a new approved version.

## 39. CRM record

CRM should record/reference:
- Primary Customer;
- Royalty Linked Member;
- relationship status;
- opportunity state;
- qualifying repeat Booking;
- Royalty Credit ID;
- frozen Programme Version;
- selected Gift reference;
- selection date;
- fulfilment hold;
- order reference;
- delivered date;
- nominee if applicable.

The full Catalogue may remain outside CRM.
