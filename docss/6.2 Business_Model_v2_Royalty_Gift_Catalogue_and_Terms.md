# Royalty Relationship Reward — Royalty Token Terms

**Business Model:** v2  
**Version:** 2.0 — Royalty Tokens (supersedes the 1.0 Gift Catalogue & Programme Terms)  
**Date:** 13 October 2026, from the owner-approved token handoff of 12 October 2026 (docss/11)

**Timezone:** Asia/Kolkata

> These Terms govern the non-cash Royalty Relationship Reward, paid in Royalty Tokens. Read them with Business Model v2 (docss/1), docss/11, the Member Terms and the transaction records. The file keeps its old name so references to "6.2" still resolve. The Gift catalogue, Programme Version and Gift fulfilment of version 1.0 are withdrawn.

## 1. Purpose

The Royalty Relationship Reward recognises one genuine long-term Customer relationship created through the Customer's qualifying first Member-sold purchase.

It is:
- non-cash;
- relationship-based;
- one-time per qualifying Customer opportunity;
- not passive income;
- not multi-level;
- not a monetary percentage.

## 2. No monetary Royalty

There are no Royalty percentage bands, positions, cycles or cash Royalty payouts.

> **One qualifying Royalty event = one Royalty award = the triggering purchase's Project token rate, in Royalty Tokens.**

## 3. Relationship creation

At the Primary Customer's earliest qualifying first purchase:

**Sold By Member:** the selling Member becomes the Provisional Royalty Linked Member.

**Sold By Customer / Sold By 3% Club:** no Member Royalty relationship is created.

A later Member sale does not retroactively create the first-purchase relationship.

## 4. Primary Customer only

Only the Primary Customer establishes the Royalty relationship. Additional Customers do not each create separate Royalty relationships, and a later Primary Customer transfer creates none.

## 5. Relationship finalisation

The provisional relationship becomes final when the first qualifying purchase reaches:
- 100% verified Payment Received; or
- a qualifying Approved Buyback (§6).

## 6. Buyback minimum source payment

Approved Buyback can act as an alternative Royalty milestone only when the source Booking has already reached:

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

## 7. Cancellation before finalisation

If the first Booking is cancelled before both 100% Payment Received and a qualifying Approved Buyback:
- the provisional relationship is removed from current entitlement;
- the opportunity is not consumed;
- a later genuine first qualifying purchase may establish the relationship.

History remains auditable.

## 8. One opportunity per Customer

Each qualifying Primary Customer creates one Royalty opportunity for life for the valid Royalty Linked Member.

## 9. Reward-triggering future purchase

The Royalty Linked Member receives one Royalty award when that Customer later makes the first qualifying direct personal purchase through 3% Club, provided:
- the opportunity is unused;
- final Sold By is not another Member;
- the purchase's Booking Request version carries a token rate;
- all other rules are satisfied.

Joining, inviting a Member, creating a referral relationship or an ordinary non-qualifying sale earns no tokens.

## 10. Another Member closes the repeat purchase

If another Member is final Sold By:
- no Royalty award is generated on that sale;
- the opportunity remains unused.

## 11. Final Sold By controls Club-direct treatment

Under the approved Owner decision:

> **The final approved Sold By selection alone decides whether the repeat purchase is Club-direct.**

Background/informal involvement by the Royalty Linked Member does not automatically reclassify the sale. Fraudulent data entry remains prohibited.

## 12. Token rate

Each Project's approved settings set the number of tokens per qualifying Royalty — a whole number. Admin prepares it and MD approves it, on the create form and in every later settings version.

The award uses the rate of the **triggering purchase's** Project, not the Project of the Customer's first purchase.

## 13. Rate freeze

The rate is saved when each Booking Request version is submitted, and Accounts approves that version with its saved rate. A later rate change does not alter a submitted or approved version. An explicit resubmission creates a new version at the then-current rate.

When the purchase qualifies later, the approved saved rate is used unchanged. No missing rate is ever substituted.

## 14. Pending, posted and restricted tokens

A qualifying event creates a pending award. Its tokens are posted to the Member's balance only when the release conditions hold:
- the Member is not deactivated and owes no Recovery;
- no staff-conflict or circumvention review is open;
- for a Buyback-qualified purchase, the purchase has reached 100% Payment Received or the Buyback has reached Stable Buyback Completion.

Pending tokens are not counted in the Member's figures. If a release condition is later lost, posted tokens stay earned but are restricted: they cannot be spent until the condition is restored. A hold never deletes earned tokens.

## 15. One balance across every Project

Tokens are one common unit. Tokens from every Project form one balance. Example: 10 tokens from Project A plus 20 from Project B make 30. No permission, transfer request or conversion is needed to combine them, and there are no Project-specific wallets.

## 16. Using tokens

Members may use part or all of their available tokens and keep the rest. Redemption arrangements are made with the Company outside the CRM. The Company then records the completed spending: Member, tokens, redemption date and recipient.

Availability is checked before the redemption is finalised and again when it is recorded. A redemption is refused while the Member is deactivated or owes a Recovery, or if it exceeds the unrestricted tokens available — even when the recorded balance is positive. A retried or duplicated submission is never a second redemption.

## 17. Recipient

Tokens may be redeemed for the Member or immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling). A redemption for one non-family recipient is recorded at once; no MD approval is needed.

The Member owns the tokens. They cannot be transferred to, pooled with or sold to another Member.

## 18. No expiry, no cash

Tokens do not expire. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn as cash. There is no automatic cash alternative.

## 19. What a Member sees

The Member portal shows only:
- **Tokens earned** — net valid posted earnings after reversals;
- **Tokens spent** — net completed spending after corrections;
- **Token balance** — earned minus spent; it may be zero or negative;

and, under Tokens spent, each redemption's tokens and date. When a hold or deficit stops spending, the portal says so.

## 20. Reversal

If the purchase that earned an award stops qualifying, its posted tokens are reversed exactly once. This covers cancellation, a payment correction below the milestone, a Buyback unwind without the normal milestone, a Sold By correction, or an identity merge that makes it a duplicate. Spending already completed is not erased.

## 21. Negative balance

A reversal may make the balance negative. Tokens earned later first cover the deficit. Spending is blocked until enough unrestricted tokens remain. No cash debt, invoice or monetary recovery arises from a token deficit.

Example: earn 30, spend 25, then a 10-token award is reversed → earned 20, spent 25, balance −5. Earn another 20 → balance 15.

## 22. Lifetime consumption

Once any token of an award has been used — towards a redemption or a deficit — that award is consumed:
- the Customer's one Royalty opportunity stays consumed, even if the award is later reversed;
- cancellation, an identity merge or a relinking never turns it into a new opportunity, and nothing transfers to another Member;
- only the same award may come back, if its own purchase legitimately qualifies again, under its original saved rate.

An award reversed before any of its tokens were used reopens the opportunity under the normal rules.

## 23. Corrections

Every correction is a linked, dated entry with a reason and the person who made it. Balances are never overwritten and history is never deleted. Correcting an erroneous redemption gives back its tokens, but does not reopen a consumed reward.

## 24. Sold By correction

A Sold By correction that touches tokens already used requires MD approval and does not generate a second award.

## 25. Recovery, deactivation and reviews

Recovery Outstanding, Member Deactivation and an open staff-conflict or circumvention review:
- keep pending awards pending;
- restrict posted tokens;
- block spending;
- preserve the relationship and token history.

## 26. Staff conflicts

A staff member's or a defined close relative's award requires conflict disclosure and MD approval. Staff cannot approve or record their own tokens.

## 27. Fraud

No award is valid for fake Customers, duplicate identity abuse, false Sold By, sham Bookings, fabricated first purchases, collusion or material misrepresentation.

## 28. Tax / statutory treatment

Applicable tax, withholding and reporting treatment follows law and professional advice. Do not advertise tokens or what they are redeemed for as tax-free or no-TDS unless professionally confirmed.

## 29. No guarantee

A Royalty relationship alone earns nothing. Future Project rates may differ; the saved rate on the qualifying Booking Request version controls the award.

## 30. CRM record

The CRM keeps:
- the Royalty relationship and Linked Member;
- each award: Customer, Member, triggering Booking and request version, Project, saved rate, state and hold;
- an append-only token ledger;
- each redemption: tokens, date, recipient and internal reference;
- which awards funded each redemption.

## 31. Member acknowledgement

> I understand that the Royalty Relationship Reward is paid in non-cash Royalty Tokens at the triggering Project's saved rate, once per qualifying Customer. A Royalty relationship alone earns nothing. Tokens do not expire, cannot be transferred and have no cash value. A reversal may make my balance negative, and later tokens first cover it.

## 32. Final principle

> **The Royalty Relationship Reward recognises one genuine long-term Customer relationship through one controlled, non-cash token award — not a recurring cash commission.**
