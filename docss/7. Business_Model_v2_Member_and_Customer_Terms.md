# 1. Purpose

These Terms govern the relationship between 3% Club / Thirty Milestones LLP ("Company", "3% Club", "we", "us") and:

1. persons applying for or holding 3% Club Membership ("Member"); and
2. persons enquiring about, buying, jointly buying, referring, closing or otherwise participating in a property transaction as customers ("Customer").

These Terms define:

- Membership conduct;
- Customer conduct;
- Project-specific commercial benefits;
- Direct Commission;
- Customer Loyalty;
- Trip Reward participation;
- Royalty Relationship Reward;
- Buying Commission where separately approved;
- money-handling restrictions;
- compliance obligations;
- KYC and payment controls;
- recovery and deactivation;
- privacy and confidentiality expectations;
- dispute / correction principles;
- programme-version acceptance.

---

# 2. Important Business Positioning

3% Club is a real-estate business membership network.

Membership is intended to support serious property professionals and relationship-rich businesspeople through selected Project opportunities, business identity, professional support and applicable Project-specific benefits.

Membership is not:

- employment;
- partnership;
- franchise unless separately documented;
- guaranteed agency appointment;
- guaranteed income;
- guaranteed lead supply;
- guaranteed sale;
- guaranteed commission;
- guaranteed investment return;
- multi-level income;
- passive-income entitlement.

Joining, inviting another person or entering an Enquiry alone creates no commission or reward.

# 6. Definitions

For these Terms:

**Active Member** means a Member whose Membership is currently active under Company records.

**Deactivated Member** means a Person who retains Member history/capability but whose Membership is currently deactivated.

**Primary Customer** means the primary named buyer recorded for the Booking.

**Additional Customer** means any additional named buyer on the Booking.

**Sold By** means the final approved transaction-closing classification:
- Sold By Member;
- Sold By Customer;
- Sold By 3% Club.

**Booking Request** means the transaction request submitted for approval.

**Approved Booking Request Version** means the version of the Booking Request that Accounts ultimately approves.

**Payment Received** means verified Customer-side sale payment progress.

**Payment Given** means verified Company-side acquisition / Buyback payment progress.

**Commissionable Sale Value** means:

> (Final Approved Base Property Value + Applicable PLC) − Authorised Discount

subject to the exclusions in Business Model v2.

**Direct Commission** means the Project-specific monetary commission payable to the qualifying Member under the applicable rules.

**Customer Loyalty** means the Project-specific monetary benefit available under either:
- Customer-closing Loyalty; or
- Repeat-purchase Loyalty.

**Own-Sale Credit** means a qualifying Trip Credit arising from a Member's eligible unit sale or eligible formal self-purchase.

**Reference Credit** means the limited Trip Credit arising from a directly introduced Member's first qualifying Reference-eligible third-party sale.

**Royalty Relationship Reward** means the one-time non-cash relationship reward governed by the applicable Royalty Gift Programme.

**Buying Commission** means the separately approved acquisition-side commission based on Acquisition Price.

**Recovery Outstanding** means a benefit already paid or fulfilled in a manner that becomes recoverable under applicable rules.

**Project Settings Version / Programme Version** means the approved version that controls the relevant Project benefit terms.

# PART A — COMMON TERMS

# 8. Accurate Information

Every Member and Customer must provide information that is true, current and complete.

No person may intentionally provide:

- false identity;
- fake Customer details;
- fake ownership details;
- false Sold By information;
- false payment information;
- sham transaction data;
- false relationship information;
- duplicate identity intended to create extra benefits.

Material misrepresentation may result in:

- rejection;
- cancellation;
- reward/commission reversal;
- Recovery;
- Membership action;
- legal action where applicable.

---

# 9. Identity and KYC

The Company may require identity, address, tax, bank or other KYC information according to:

- transaction stage;
- payout stage;
- statutory requirement;
- risk review;
- specific programme requirement.

General buyer KYC may be completed later in the Booking lifecycle where permitted by Company policy.

However:

> a Customer who wishes to earn Customer-closing Loyalty must satisfy the specific Customer-closer KYC requirements before that Loyalty entitlement is approved.

No monetary payout is released until required beneficiary verification is complete.

---

# 10. PAN / Aadhaar / Identity Data

Where PAN, Aadhaar or other identity information is collected:

- it must be handled under the Company's approved privacy/security policy;
- access should be limited to authorised users;
- duplicate checks may be performed;
- statutory handling requirements apply.

Full Aadhaar visibility, storage and access should follow the Company's final legally reviewed privacy/data-storage policy.

---

# 11. Bank Verification

Monetary benefits require an approved verified bank account.

Normal rule:

> one verified bank account is associated with one Person.

A genuine joint-account exception may be accepted only with appropriate proof and authorised approval.

The Company may hold payment while bank ownership or beneficiary identity is unclear.

---

# 12. Customer Payment Channels

Customer money must be paid only through authorised payment channels communicated by the Company / applicable Project party.

Members and Customer closers must not:

- collect Customer money in their personal account;
- issue unofficial receipts;
- ask a Customer to pay an unauthorised person;
- represent a personal receipt as an official Company receipt.

A Customer should verify the authorised payment recipient before paying.

---

# 13. Third-Party Payments

Where a payment is made by a person other than the named buyer, the Company may require:

- payer identity;
- relationship to buyer;
- payment reference;
- source-of-funds clarification;
- additional declaration or approval.

A third-party payment does not automatically change ownership or benefit entitlement.

---

# 14. Authorised Discounts

Only an authorised and properly recorded discount changes the formal Commissionable Sale Value.

No Member or Customer closer may promise an unauthorised:

- discount;
- cashback;
- free upgrade;
- waiver;
- rebate;
- payment adjustment.

Any concession must be approved and recorded through the authorised process.

---

# 15. No Unauthorised Representations

Members and Customer closers must not claim that:

- title is guaranteed;
- appreciation is guaranteed;
- resale is guaranteed;
- Company Buyback is guaranteed;
- possession/registry will occur on a guaranteed date unless authorised documentation says so;
- Membership guarantees income;
- Project Commission is always 3%;
- Trip or Gift is guaranteed before qualification;
- tax/TDS will not apply;
- any oral statement overrides written approved Project terms.

---

# 16. Official Receipts and Written Terms

Customers should rely on:

- authorised written Project documents;
- approved Booking details;
- official receipts / payment references;
- applicable Terms;
- authorised Company communications.

Unauthorised oral promises by a Member, Customer closer or other person do not automatically bind the Company.

---

# 17. Project Inventory

Availability of a Plot/Unit is subject to the Company's approved inventory, Hold and Booking process.

An Enquiry does not reserve inventory.

A Hold does not equal an approved Booking.

A Booking Request does not become an approved Booking until the required approval occurs.

---

# 18. Sold By Classification

Each sale has one final approved Sold By classification:

- Sold By Member;
- Sold By Customer;
- Sold By 3% Club.

A single sale cannot generate both Project Direct Commission and Customer Loyalty to different closers.

The approved Sold By classification controls the sale-side monetary route.

---

# 19. Corrections

If information is entered incorrectly, the Company may use a controlled correction workflow.

Corrections may affect:

- Sold By;
- beneficiary;
- Customer identity;
- payment progress;
- Direct;
- Loyalty;
- Trip Credit;
- Reference Credit;
- Royalty relationship;
- Royalty Reward.

Corrections must retain audit/history and may require Admin or MD approval.

---

# 20. Version Freeze

The applicable commercial/reward version is the:

> **Booking Request version that Accounts ultimately approves.**

A rejected or abandoned request freezes no commercial entitlement.

A materially edited/resubmitted request is governed by the version applicable to the request that is ultimately approved.

Approved Bookings retain their frozen version even if Project settings later change.

---

# 21. No Retroactive Reward Improvement

A later:

- rate increase;
- Trip activation;
- target reduction;
- Gift improvement;
- inventory change

does not automatically improve an earlier Booking that froze under different terms.

---

Any tax, withholding, TDS, GST, reporting or other statutory treatment applies according to applicable law and professional advice.

- any commission is tax-free;
- any Gift is tax-free;

Current launch policy may provide:

> **Membership Application submission**

Member status may include:

# 35. Member Project Direct Commission

Direct Commission is Project-specific.

A Project may have Direct:

- Enabled; or
- Disabled.

Hard maximum:

> **5%**

The applicable rate is the frozen Project rate for the approved Booking Request version.

---

# 36. Third-Party Member Sale Direct Milestone

For an eligible third-party Member sale:

> **full Direct eligibility occurs at 25% verified Payment Received.**

If the transaction later becomes invalid:

- unpaid Direct may be cancelled;
- paid Direct may become Recovery Outstanding.

---

# 37. Formal Member Self-Purchase

A Booking is formally treated as Member self-purchase only when:

> **the Primary Customer is an Active Member.**

For formal Member self-purchase:

- buyer-Member may receive the Project Direct rate if Enabled;
- Direct milestone = 100% Payment Received;
- no second selling Member Direct;
- no Customer Loyalty;
- eligible self-purchase may count toward Own-Sale Trip progress;
- inviter receives no Reference Credit merely from the self-purchase;
- Buyback does not accelerate Direct.

---

# 38. Related-Party Member Sales

Business Model v2 does not automatically treat a related-party purchase as self-purchase.

A Member may sell to a spouse, parent, child, sibling or another related/associated person under normal third-party rules if:

- formal self-purchase classification is not triggered;
- transaction is genuine;
- information is correctly recorded.

Fraud/sham arrangements remain prohibited.

---

# 39. Member Sales & Reference Trip Reward

A Member may participate in an applicable Project Trip Programme subject to:

- Trip Programme = Enabled;
- eligible inventory;
- applicable Programme Version;
- qualification milestones;
- Trip Programme Terms.

Trip reward details are governed by:

> **Business Model v2 — Sales & Reference Trip Reward Programme Terms**

Those Terms are incorporated by reference when the Member participates.

---

# 40. Own-Sale Credits

Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may create one Own-Sale Credit.

One Member may earn at most one Own-Sale Credit for the same Plot/Unit within the same Project Trip Programme across all sale types.

---

# 41. Reference Credits

A directly introduced Member can contribute one lifetime Reference Credit to the immediate inviter.

There is:

- no joining credit;
- no multi-level credit;
- no Reference Credit merely because a Member was introduced.

The specific first-sale and Trip Programme rules apply.

---

# 42. Trip Credit Expiry

Unused Qualified Trip Credits expire:

> **12 months after qualification**

subject to the applicable Project Trip Programme and closure/wind-down terms.

---

# 43. Trip Reward Nominee

The earning Member remains owner of the Reward.

Before fulfilment, the Member may nominate:

- immediate family; or
- one non-family nominee with MD approval.

No automatic cash alternative exists.

---

# 44. Royalty Relationship Reward

A Member may become the Royalty Linked Member only under the applicable first-purchase relationship rule.

Royalty is:

> **non-cash**

and is governed by:

> **Business Model v2 — Royalty Relationship Reward Gift Catalogue & Programme Terms**

One qualifying Royalty event creates:

> one Royalty Credit = one Catalogue Gift entitlement.

There are no monetary Royalty percentage bands or cycles.

---

# 45. Royalty Relationship Does Not Guarantee a Gift

A Royalty relationship alone does not earn a Gift.

The future qualifying transaction and all eligibility/fulfilment rules must be satisfied.

---

# 46. Buying Commission

Buying Commission is separate from Direct, Loyalty, Trip and Royalty Reward.

Where separately approved:

- rate is based on Acquisition Price;
- hard maximum = 5%;
- one beneficiary applies;
- rate freezes at formal Acquisition approval;
- normal milestone = 100% Payment Given;
- Paid Early requires MD approval.

A participant in the original sale is not automatically prohibited from separately earning Buying Commission on a later Buyback, provided the approved Buying Commission rules are satisfied.

The returning owner cannot earn Buying Commission merely for arranging their own return.

---

# 47. Deactivated Member

While Deactivated, a Member:

- may buy property as a normal Customer;
- may have another genuine Member sell to them;

but cannot personally earn:

- Customer-closing Loyalty;
- repeat-purchase Loyalty.

Member reward fulfilment may also be held until valid reactivation.

---

# 48. Recovery Outstanding

If a paid benefit later becomes invalid, the Company may record Recovery Outstanding.

Recovery may be:

- demanded from the beneficiary;
- set off against future monetary benefits;
- linked to an Accounts Recovery Reference;
- used as a basis to hold future payout or reward fulfilment.

Normal recovery deadline:

> **15 calendar days from notice**

subject to applicable law and final operational/legal terms.

---

# 49. Membership Deactivation for Recovery / Compliance

Unresolved Recovery or material compliance breach may result in Membership deactivation.

Deactivation does not erase:

- historical transactions;
- Recovery;
- earned history;
- audit records.

Reactivation may require:

- Recovery Cleared;
- compliance resolved;
- authorised approval.

---

# 50. Reward Fulfilment Hold

Recovery Outstanding or Deactivation may hold:

- Trip fulfilment;
- Royalty Gift fulfilment;

without deleting the recorded entitlement.

---

# 51. Staff / Conflict-of-Interest Cases

If a staff member or defined close relative is also eligible for a Member benefit:

- conflict disclosure is required;
- independent approval is required;
- MD approval may be required;
- staff cannot approve their own benefit.

---

# PART C — CUSTOMER TERMS

# 52. Customer Role

A Customer may:

- enquire;
- Hold a Plot/Unit;
- submit a Booking Request;
- buy individually or jointly;
- make repeat personal purchases;
- in eligible cases close a sale for another buyer;
- later apply for Membership.

Customer status does not automatically create a right to Customer Loyalty.

---

# 53. Primary and Additional Customer

One person is recorded as Primary Customer.

Additional Customers may also be named.

The Primary Customer is the key Customer identity for:

- formal Member self-purchase classification;
- first personal purchase / repeat purchase history;
- Royalty relationship creation.

---

# 54. Customer-Closer Eligibility

To earn Customer-closing Loyalty, the Sold By Customer must be a real existing Customer.

Required:

- their own approved personal purchase;
- verified KYC;
- accepted Customer Terms;
- compliance with money-handling and marketing restrictions.

A random person cannot be nominated merely to receive Customer Loyalty.

---

# 55. Customer-Closer Conduct

A Customer closer must:

- provide true closing information;
- not collect buyer money personally;
- not issue unofficial receipts;
- not make unauthorised Project promises;
- not misrepresent the Company;
- comply with applicable law;
- use approved information.

---

# 56. Customer-Closing Loyalty

Customer-closing Loyalty is Project-specific.

Project Loyalty may be:

- Enabled; or
- Disabled.

Hard maximum:

> **3%**

A qualifying Customer closer may receive the frozen Project Loyalty rate.

Normal milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

A qualifying Approved Buyback may act as an alternative milestone only under the 25% source-payment safeguard.

---

# 57. Customer-Closing Lifetime Limit

Customer-closing Loyalty is limited to:

> **3 successful Customer-closing Loyalty events for life.**

After the third successful Customer-closing event:

> the person must become an Active Member to continue earning from future third-party selling activity.

Repeat-purchase Loyalty remains separate and may continue if otherwise eligible.

---

# 58. Repeat-Purchase Loyalty

A qualifying repeat personal purchase may earn the applicable Project Loyalty rate.

Rules:

- first personal purchase does not earn repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- second and later qualifying personal Booking may qualify;
- same-day second/subsequent Booking may qualify;
- Project Loyalty must be Enabled;
- Member-closed repeat purchase does not generate Customer Loyalty;
- normal milestone = 100% Payment Received;
- qualifying Buyback may act as alternative milestone.

Repeat-purchase Loyalty is:

> **unlimited in lifetime quantity**

subject to continued eligibility.

---

# 59. Customer Closer May Be Related / Additional Customer

Under Business Model v2, a Customer closer is not automatically disqualified merely because the closer is:

- related to a buyer; or
- an Additional Customer on the same Booking.

All other eligibility and anti-fraud rules still apply.

---

# 60. Reciprocal Customer Closing

Customers may close sales for one another if each transaction is genuine and the relevant Customer-closing rules are satisfied.

No automatic reciprocal-pair prohibition applies.

Fraud, sham arrangements or false attribution remain prohibited.

---

# 61. Deactivated Member Buying as Customer

A Deactivated Member may buy as a normal Customer for purchase classification.

However, while Deactivated, that person cannot personally earn either Customer Loyalty route.

---

# 62. Customer → Member Conversion

A Customer may apply for Membership.

Past valid Customer benefits remain historical.

Once Active as a Member:

- future selling activity follows Member rules;
- Customer-closing Loyalty does not apply to the Active Member's selling activity.

---

# 63. Customer Royalty Relationship

Only the Primary Customer can establish a Royalty relationship from a Booking.

If the Customer's earliest qualifying first purchase is Sold By Member, the selling Member may become the Provisional Royalty Linked Member.

If the earliest qualifying first purchase is Sold By Customer or 3% Club:

> no Member Royalty relationship is created from that first purchase.

A later Member sale does not retroactively create one.

---

# 64. Future Royalty Event

Where a valid Royalty relationship exists, the Customer's first qualifying future direct personal purchase through 3% Club may create one non-cash Royalty Reward for the Royalty Linked Member.

If another Member is final Sold By on that repeat purchase:

- no Royalty Reward is generated on that sale;
- the unused opportunity remains available, subject to the Programme rules.

---

# 65. Club-Direct Classification

For the applicable repeat purchase:

> final approved Sold By determines whether the transaction is treated as Club-direct.

Background or informal involvement by a prior Member does not automatically reclassify the sale.

False Sold By information remains prohibited.

---

# 66. Customer Cancellation / Refund

Cancellation and refund rights depend on:

- applicable Project documents;
- Booking stage;
- payment stage;
- statutory rights;
- approved cancellation/refund policy;
- specific transaction documents.

These Terms do not create a universal refund amount or deadline.

The Customer should rely on the applicable written cancellation/refund terms.

---

# 67. Buyback Is Separate from Cancellation

Company Buyback / Acquisition is a separate transaction from ordinary cancellation.

No Customer has an automatic right to Buyback unless separately offered/approved.

A Buyback must follow the Company's separate approval and acquisition process.

---

# 68. Change Plot

Change Plot is subject to the approved workflow.

Where permitted:

- same Booking may continue;
- applicable frozen commercial rate/version remains;
- final valid Unit controls current transaction economics and Trip eligibility under Business Model v2;
- overpaid benefits may create Recovery.

Cross-Project movement normally requires cancellation/new Booking under the approved process.

---

# 69. Primary Customer Change

An approved Primary Customer Change changes customer ownership/details under the approved workflow.

Under the approved Business Model v2 rule, this does not automatically re-run the original benefit classification/economics.

Any separate Sold By or benefit correction must use its own controlled correction process.

---

# 70. Customer Recovery

Where a Customer has received a monetary benefit that later becomes invalid, the Company may create Recovery Outstanding.

Unresolved Recovery may:

- block future benefit payout;
- block Membership activation;
- require authorised clearance before future benefit release.

---

# PART D — BENEFIT / REWARD CONTROLS

# 71. Project-Specific Benefit Availability

A Project may have any MD-approved combination such as:

- Direct Enabled / Loyalty Enabled / Trip Enabled;
- Direct Enabled / Loyalty Disabled / Trip Enabled;
- Direct Disabled / Loyalty Enabled / Trip Disabled;
- Direct Disabled / Loyalty Disabled / Trip Enabled.

Where Direct is Disabled while Loyalty is Enabled, or Loyalty is equal to/higher than Direct, an approved MD commercial exception is required internally.

---

# 72. Benefit Calculation and Rounding

Where monetary benefits are calculated:

- each benefit is calculated separately;
- final amounts use 2 decimal places;
- no unnecessary intermediate rounding should change the outcome;
- applicable statutory deductions may be handled separately.

---

# 73. Payout Timing

Subject to:

- eligibility;
- KYC;
- verified bank;
- Recovery;
- compliance holds;
- Accounts processing;

the operational target for eligible monetary payout may be:

> within 7 working days after eligibility and holds are clear.

This is an operational target, not a guaranteed statutory payment date unless the final approved Terms expressly make it contractual.

---

# 74. Paid Early

Where a benefit type permits Paid Early, it requires:

- MD approval;
- reason;
- beneficiary;
- rate/type;
- reference;
- date/time;
- audit.

Paid Early does not create duplicate normal payout later.

If the transaction becomes invalid, Recovery may apply.

---

# 75. Buyback 25% Reward Gate

Approved Buyback can act as an alternative milestone for:

- Customer Loyalty;
- Own-Sale Trip Credit;
- Reference Credit;
- Royalty Reward eligibility

only if the source Booking has reached:

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

Buyback does not accelerate Direct Commission.

---

# 76. Stable Buyback Completion

Where Trip/Gift fulfilment depends on Buyback:

Stable Buyback Completion requires:

1. formal Acquisition approval;
2. 100% verified Payment Given;
3. no cancellation/unwind;
4. applicable acquisition completion/document-return requirements completed.

---

# 77. No Duplicate Benefit

The system/business should not create duplicate benefits from:

- repeated milestone crossing;
- payment correction;
- duplicate identity;
- repeated same-unit Trip farming;
- repeated Royalty event;
- correction after fulfilment.

---

# 78. Fulfilled Reward Finality

Once:

- a Trip is travelled; or
- a Royalty Gift is delivered,

the Credits/opportunity used for that fulfilled Reward remain consumed.

A later ordinary transaction cancellation does not automatically reopen the same Reward opportunity.

Fraud/recovery rights remain.

---

# PART E — MEMBERSHIP / CUSTOMER STATUS, SUSPENSION & TERMINATION

# 79. Company Right to Review

The Company may review Membership, Customer-closing eligibility, payout or reward fulfilment where there is a reasonable concern relating to:

- identity;
- KYC;
- payment source;
- fraud;
- Recovery;
- false attribution;
- staff conflict;
- duplicate bank use;
- related-party Recovery circumvention;
- statutory compliance.

A review does not itself establish wrongdoing.

---

# 80. Recovery Circumvention Review

If a Person connected to someone with unresolved Recovery shares indicators such as:

- bank account;
- mobile;
- address;
- known close relationship;

the Company may hold benefits for a Recovery Circumvention Review.

The connected person is not automatically liable merely because of the relationship.
