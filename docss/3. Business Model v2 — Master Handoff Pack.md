# 1. Purpose

Business Model v2 defines the final approved commercial framework for:

- Project Direct Commission;
- Customer Loyalty;
- Sales & Reference Trip Reward;
- Royalty Relationship Reward;
- Buying Commission;
- Buyback-related reward treatment;
- Project settings and versioning;
- qualification and fulfilment milestones;
- recovery, deactivation and corrections;
- accepted commercial risks;
- production activation.

No other working document may silently override this document.

Where Project-specific values vary, this document defines the framework and the approved Project Settings / Programme Version defines the actual Project values.

# 2. Business Positioning

3% Club is:

> **a real-estate business membership network for serious property professionals and relationship-rich local businesspeople.**

The business helps Members grow through:

- selected Project opportunities;
- professional identity;
- business support;
- Project-specific Direct Commission;
- structured sales-performance rewards;
- relationship-based rewards;
- continued value from genuine Customer relationships.

The public proposition is not:

- a CRM;
- software;
- passive income;
- MLM;
- downline;

The CRM supports the business. It is not the public product.

# 10. Final Sold By Classification

The final approved Sold By selection controls sale-side monetary treatment.

# 11. Direct vs Loyalty Hard Rule

The old combined 4% sale-side monetary cap is removed.

However:

> **A single sale cannot generate both Project Direct Commission and Customer Loyalty to different closers.**

| Final classification | Monetary treatment |
|---|---|
| Sold By Member | Project Direct Commission |
| Formal Member self-purchase | Buyer-Member Project Direct Commission |
| Sold By Customer | Customer Loyalty |
| Sold By 3% Club + eligible repeat personal purchase | Customer Loyalty |

Trip and Royalty rewards are non-cash and may coexist only when their own rules qualify.

Buying Commission remains a separate acquisition-side benefit.

# 13. Project Rate Limits

> Customer Loyalty should be lower than Project Direct Commission.

- Direct is Disabled and Loyalty is Enabled; or
- Loyalty is equal to or higher than Direct,

the Project may still proceed only with:

> **explicit MD exception + written commercial reason + audit trail.**

# 16. No Backdated Project Versions

A Project Settings Version:

> **cannot become effective earlier than its approval time.**

Every approved Booking permanently stores the exact frozen Version ID.

The CRM must not later re-determine the applicable version merely by comparing dates.

---

# 17. Final Booking Freeze Rule

The commercial/reward version that applies is:

> **the Booking Request version that Accounts ultimately approves.**

Rules:

- rejected request freezes nothing;
- abandoned request freezes nothing;
- materially edited/resubmitted request uses the submission timestamp/version of the request that Accounts finally approves;
- approved Booking permanently stores the exact frozen Project Settings Version ID.

---

# 18. No Retroactive Eligibility

A Booking/Unit receives only the programme/rate rules frozen on its approved Booking Request version.

Later:

- Direct increase;
- Loyalty increase;
- Trip activation;
- target reduction;
- Gift improvement;
- inventory expansion

does not retroactively improve an earlier Booking.

---

# 19. Commissionable Sale Value

Global formula:

> **Commissionable Sale Value = (Final Approved Base Property Value + Applicable PLC) − Authorised Discount**

Include:

- final approved Base Property Value;
- applicable PLC.

Exclude:

- GST/tax;
- stamp duty;
- registration;
- government levy;
- documentation;
- maintenance;
- utilities;
- finance charges;
- interest;
- penalties;
- refunds;
- pass-through amounts.

Only formally recorded **Authorised Discount** reduces the commission base.

---

# 20. Accepted Discount Risk

Cashback, free upgrade or another off-book concession does not automatically reduce Commissionable Sale Value unless it is formally recorded as Authorised Discount.

This is a conscious Owner-approved commercial risk.

---

# 21. Project Direct — Third-Party Member Sale

When:

- Member is Active;
- final Sold By = Member;
- Project Direct is Enabled;
- transaction is otherwise eligible;

the selling Member earns:

> **the frozen Project Direct rate.**

Eligibility milestone:

> **full Direct entitlement at 25% verified Payment Received.**

Direct may be earned on unlimited qualifying third-party sales.

Only one selling Member receives Direct on one sale.

---

# 22. Direct at 25% — Protection Rules

The Owner has deliberately retained full Direct eligibility at 25%.

Therefore:

- unpaid Direct is cancelled if the entitlement later becomes invalid;
- paid invalid Direct becomes Recovery Outstanding;
- Recovery may be set off against future monetary benefits owed to that Person;
- Accounts Recovery Reference must be recorded;
- unusual third-party payment sources may be reviewed;
- third-party payer identity/reference should be recorded when relevant.

No staged Direct payout is required.

---

# 23. Formal Member Self-Purchase

A Booking is treated as Member self-purchase only when:

> **the Primary Customer is an Active Member.**

An Additional Customer who is a Member does not automatically make the Booking a self-purchase.

Related-party purchases are not automatically treated as Member self-purchase.

For formal Member self-purchase:

- buyer-Member receives Project Direct if Enabled;
- frozen Project Direct rate applies;
- Direct milestone = **100% verified Payment Received**;
- no second selling Member Direct;
- no Customer Loyalty;
- self-purchase may create Own-Sale Trip Credits;
- inviter receives no Reference Credit merely from the self-purchase;
- Buyback does not accelerate Direct.

---

# 24. Related-Party Member Sales — Accepted Rule

A Member may sell to:

- spouse;
- parent;
- child;
- sibling;
- other related person;
- associated person/entity,

and still receive normal third-party sale treatment if the formal self-purchase rule is not triggered.

This is intentional.

Fraud, sham and collusive transactions remain prohibited.

---

# 25. Customer Loyalty — Two Routes

Customer Loyalty has two routes:

1. **Customer-closing Loyalty**
2. **Repeat-personal-purchase Loyalty**

Both use the applicable frozen Project Loyalty rate.

---

# 26. Customer-Closer Eligibility

A Sold By Customer must be a real existing Customer.

Required:

- their own approved personal purchase exists;
- verified KYC;
- accepted Customer Terms;
- Customer Terms bind them to:
  - authorised payment handling;
  - no unofficial receipts;
  - no unauthorised promises;
  - applicable marketing restrictions;
  - programme terms.

A random non-Member Person cannot be used merely as a Customer closer.

---

# 27. Customer-Closing Loyalty Limit

Customer-closing Loyalty is limited to:

> **3 successful Customer-closing Loyalty events for life.**

After the third successful Customer-closing Loyalty event:

> the person must become an Active Member to continue earning from future third-party selling activity.

This limit applies only to Customer-closing activity.

---

# 28. Customer-Closing Loyalty Milestone

Normal milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Approved Buyback may act as an alternative milestone only if the source Booking satisfies the Buyback minimum-payment rule.

---

# 29. Repeat-Purchase Loyalty

Repeat-purchase Loyalty remains:

> **unlimited in lifetime quantity.**

Rules:

- first personal purchase never earns repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- second and later qualifying personal Booking may qualify;
- Project Loyalty must be Enabled;
- final sale treatment must qualify;
- Member-closed repeat purchase does not generate Customer Loyalty;
- normal milestone = 100% Payment Received;
- Approved Buyback may qualify under the Buyback safeguard.

---

# 30. Immediate Repeat Purchase — Accepted Rule

A second qualifying personal Booking may count as a repeat purchase immediately.

No 30-day gap or other minimum waiting period is required.

Same-day second/subsequent Bookings may therefore qualify.

This is an intentional accepted risk.

---

# 31. Sold By Customer May Be Related or Co-Buyer — Accepted Rule

A Sold By Customer may be:

- related to a buyer; and/or
- an Additional Customer on the same Booking,

provided they satisfy the Customer-closer eligibility rules.

No automatic disqualification applies merely because of that relationship.

---

# 32. Reciprocal Customer Closing — Accepted Rule

Friends or unrelated Customers may close sales for each other.

No automatic reciprocal-pair block is required.

Each closer must still:

- satisfy Customer-closer eligibility;
- remain within the 3 lifetime Customer-closing limit;
- meet all other transaction rules.

---

# 33. Active Member and Customer Loyalty

An Active Member cannot use Customer-closing Loyalty for their own selling activity.

Future activity while Active uses Member rules.

---

# 34. Deactivated Member

A Deactivated Member:

- may buy property as a normal Customer;
- may be treated as Customer for purchase classification;
- may have another genuine Member sell to them under normal Member-sale rules;

but while Deactivated cannot personally earn:

- Customer-closing Loyalty;
- repeat-purchase Loyalty.

Existing protected transactions submitted while properly eligible retain frozen treatment.

---

# 35. Deactivated Member Purchase Effect

Because purchase classification is treated as normal Customer purchase:

- another Member may earn normal eligible Direct;
- normal Royalty relationship rules may apply;
- no special self-purchase 100% Direct milestone applies merely because the buyer was previously a Member.

---

# 36. Primary Customer Change — Accepted Rule

Approved Primary Customer Change:

> **changes ownership/customer details only.**

It does not automatically re-run:

- original Direct classification;
- original Loyalty classification;
- original formal self-purchase classification;
- original reward economics.

Any separate correction uses the appropriate controlled correction workflow.

This is an intentional accepted risk.

---

# 37. Customer → Member Conversion

A Customer may apply to become a Member at any time.

If a valid inviter exists:

> inviter freezes at Membership Application submission.

Later correction requires:

- Admin/MD authority;
- reason;
- audit trail.

If none exists, no inviter is assigned.

A previous selling Member or Royalty Linked Member is not automatically the Membership inviter.

---

# 38. Late Inviter Correction Limit

No inviter may be added or changed for Reference purposes after:

> **the introduced Member's first Reference-eligible Booking Request is submitted.**

This prevents last-minute creation of Reference entitlement.

---

# 39. Sales & Reference Trip Reward

The Trip Programme rewards:

1. genuine Project unit sales;
2. genuine first qualifying sale performance of directly introduced Members.

It is not a joining reward.

It is not multi-level.

Joining creates:

> **zero Trip progress.**

---

# 40. Trip Programme Settings

Each Trip-enabled Project defines:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- eligible inventory;
- excluded inventory;
- Programme Version;
- Trip Terms/PPT Version.

Example:

> Target 9 / Minimum Own 6 / Maximum Reference 3

Eligible:

- 9 Own + 0 Reference
- 8 Own + 1 Reference
- 7 Own + 2 Reference
- 6 Own + 3 Reference

Not eligible:

- 5 Own + 4 Reference

Reference is optional.

---

# 41. Own-Sale Credit — Unit/Plot Based

> **Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may create one Own-Sale Credit.**

Five qualifying units in one Booking may create five Own-Sale Credits.

Credit is based on the unit, not Booking-number structure.

---

# 42. Member Self-Purchase Trip Credit

Formal Member self-purchase may generate Own-Sale Credit.

There is no general cap on how many genuine self-purchased units may satisfy Own-Sale requirements.

The same Member/unit/programme anti-recycling rule applies.

---

# 43. Same Member + Same Unit Anti-Recycling

Across all sale types:

> **one Member may earn at most one Own-Sale Credit for the same Plot/Unit in the same Project Trip Programme.**

This applies to:

- self-purchase;
- third-party sale;
- resale after Buyback.

A different Member may earn a later genuine Own-Sale Credit for the same unit.

---

# 44. Subdivided Unit Rule

If an eligible unit is subdivided after programme activation:

> the child units share one Trip Credit pool from the parent unit

unless MD activates a new Programme Version explicitly recognising them as separate eligible credit units.

---

# 45. Change Plot — Trip Rule

If Booking changes Plot:

> the final valid unit must be eligible under the Booking's frozen Trip Programme version.

If final unit is excluded:

> pending/qualified Trip Credit reverses.

---

# 46. Normal Trip Qualification

Normal milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Before qualification:

> Pending Trip Credit

At qualification:

> Qualified Trip Credit

---

# 47. Trip Bucket Open / Freeze

A Trip bucket opens only when:

> **the eligible Booking Request version is approved by Accounts and creates the Member's first valid Pending Trip Credit in that Project.**

A rejected/raw request does not open a bucket.

At opening, freeze:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- self-purchase rules;
- eligible inventory;
- Trip Programme Version;
- Trip Terms/PPT Version.

After the bucket is earned/closed, the next bucket uses the then-current Project Trip Programme Version.

---

# 48. Project Trip Programme Closure

When a Trip Programme closes:

- no post-cut-off approved Booking enters;
- protected pre-cut-off approved activity continues under its frozen version;
- Project Terms define the final wind-down/completion deadline.

Partial progress is not silently removed before the disclosed deadline.

---

# 49. Reference Credit — Core Rule

Rules:

- immediate inviter only;
- no multi-level;
- one directly introduced Member can contribute **one lifetime Reference Credit**;
- self-purchase by the introduced Member does not consume the Reference opportunity;
- opportunity is global across Projects;
- it is consumed only by the first Reference-eligible third-party sale in an active Trip Programme.

A sale in a Project with no active Trip Programme does not consume the opportunity.

---

# 50. First Referred Sale with Multiple Units

If introduced Member B's first Reference-eligible transaction contains multiple qualifying units:

- B may earn multiple Own-Sale Credits;
- B's immediate inviter receives **one Reference Credit only**.

---

# 51. Reference Credit Qualification

Reference Credit qualifies when the introduced Member's first Reference-eligible third-party sale reaches:

- 100% verified Payment Received; or
- Approved Buyback alternative milestone satisfying the minimum-payment safeguard.

If multiple candidate sales qualify at exactly the same time:

1. earliest verified qualification timestamp wins;
2. if equal, lower permanent Booking Number wins.

---

# 52. Reference Project Steering — Accepted Rule

Because qualification timing determines the first Reference-eligible event, payment timing may influence which Project receives the one lifetime Reference Credit.

This is an intentional accepted low-risk commercial choice.

---

# 53. One Sale May Create Seller + Inviter Trip Credits

A first qualifying referred sale may separately create:

- Own-Sale Credit(s) for the selling Member; and
- one Reference Credit for the immediate inviter.

These are separate entitlements.

---

# 54. Extra Reference Credits

If a current bucket cannot use all qualified Reference Credits:

> extra qualified Reference Credits remain banked for that same Project.

They may be used in later buckets subject to:

- later bucket composition;
- FIFO;
- credit expiry.

---

# 55. Deactivated Inviter

If the introduced Member qualifies the one lifetime Reference event while the inviter is Deactivated:

- Reference opportunity is consumed;
- Reference Credit is created;
- credit use/fulfilment is held until valid reactivation.

---

# 56. Trip Credit Expiry

Unused Qualified Trip Credits expire:

> **12 months after qualification.**

Project Terms must also define:

> final completion/wind-down deadline for any open bucket after programme closure.

Already Earned Trip Rewards follow their separate redemption window.

---

# 57. FIFO Allocation

Trip Credits are allocated:

> **earliest qualified eligible credits first**

subject to:

- credit type;
- Minimum Own requirement;
- Maximum Reference allowance;
- frozen programme rules.

Used credits cannot be reused.

---

# 58. Repeatable Trips

When a Member completes a Trip bucket:

- Trip Reward becomes Earned;
- used credits lock;
- fresh/unused eligible credits may build the next bucket.

Each fresh completed target may earn another Trip while the applicable Programme remains available under its terms.

---

# 59. Approved Buyback — Minimum Payment Safeguard

Approved Buyback can act as an alternative reward milestone only if the source Booking has already reached:

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

before Buyback qualifies the relevant reward.

This applies to Buyback-based qualification for:

- Customer Loyalty;
- Own-Sale Trip Credit;
- Reference Credit;
- Royalty Reward eligibility.

Buyback does not accelerate Direct Commission.

---

# 60. Buyback-Qualified Trip Credit — Owner

The credit belongs to the source sale:

- source Sold By Member → source selling Member gets Own-Sale Credit(s);
- eligible first referred sale → immediate inviter may get one Reference Credit;
- Member self-purchase source → eligible Own-Sale Credit subject to anti-recycling;
- Buying Commission beneficiary gets no Trip Credit merely for arranging the Buyback.

Ordinary cancellation does not create Trip Credit.

---

# 61. Stable Buyback Completion

A Buyback-qualified Trip Credit may become Qualified at Approved Buyback, but becomes usable for Trip fulfilment only after:

> **Stable Buyback Completion**

Stable Buyback Completion means all applicable conditions are true:

1. Buyback/Acquisition formally Approved;
2. Payment Given = 100% verified;
3. no cancellation/unwind;
4. applicable completion/document-return requirements completed, including where relevant:
   - Allotment papers collected back;
   - Registry-back completed;
   - other required acquisition completion conditions.

---

# 62. Buyback Unwind

If Buyback later unwinds before Stable Buyback Completion:

- Buyback-based reward qualification reverses;
- source-sale status is recalculated;
- if source sale later independently satisfies the normal milestone, normal qualification may apply;
- no duplicate credit is created.

---

# 63. Trip Cancellation / Reversal

## Before Reward Earned
Invalid credit reverses and progress recalculates.

## Earned but Not Booked
If a used credit becomes invalid:

- reward may become Deficient;
- next unused eligible qualified credit may backfill under FIFO;
- otherwise fulfilment pauses.

## Trip Booked
Published Trip Terms control:

- cancellation;
- rebooking;
- refundability;
- traveller changes;
- Company cancellation.

## Travel Completed
No ordinary business-event clawback.

Fraud, sham activity or material misrepresentation remains separately actionable.

---

# 64. Fulfilled Reward Finality

Once:

- Trip is travelled; or
- Royalty Gift is delivered,

all underlying credits/opportunities used for that fulfilled reward remain:

> **consumed forever**

even if the underlying sale later cancels.

Fraud/recovery may still be pursued, but the old opportunity does not reopen to create another reward.

---

# 65. Trip Reward Ownership and Nominee

The earning Member remains the owner of:

# 114. Owner-Accepted Commercial Risks

The following are not accidental omissions. They were reviewed and deliberately accepted.

## 114.1 Related-party Member sales
A Member may sell to relatives/related persons and still receive normal third-party treatment where the formal self-purchase rule is not triggered.

**Example:** Member A sells to A's brother and may still earn normal Direct.

## 114.2 Sold By Customer may be related or an Additional Customer
Customer closer is not automatically disqualified merely because they are related to a buyer or are an Additional Customer.

**Example:** Wife is Additional Customer and Sold By Customer on Husband's Booking, subject to Customer-closer eligibility.

## 114.3 Primary Customer Change does not re-run original economics
Ownership/customer change alone does not automatically rewrite original benefit classification.

**Example:** Direct paid on the original approved sale is not automatically recalculated merely because Primary Customer later changes.

## 114.4 Immediate repeat-purchase Loyalty
No minimum time gap exists between first and repeat purchase.

**Example:** Customer buys Plot 1 in the morning and Plot 2 later the same day; Plot 2 may qualify as repeat purchase.

## 114.5 Reciprocal Customer closing
Friends/Customers may close sales for each other.

**Example:** A closes B's purchase; later B closes A's purchase, subject to each remaining eligible and within the 3-event limit.

## 114.6 Full Direct at 25%
Full Direct remains eligible at 25% Payment Received.

**Example:** On a 5% Direct Project, Member becomes eligible for the full Direct amount once the sale reaches 25% Payment Received; later cancellation relies on Recovery controls.

## 114.7 Formal Authorised Discount only
Off-book economic concessions do not automatically reduce the commission base.

**Example:** Separate cashback does not reduce Commissionable Sale Value unless formally entered as Authorised Discount.

## 114.8 General KYC before payout
General buyer KYC is not universally required before Booking approval.

**Example:** Booking may be approved while payout-grade KYC is still pending, subject to existing Booking rules.

## 114.9 Final Sold By controls Club-direct repeat purchase
Background Member involvement does not automatically override final Sold By.

**Example:** Original Member informally discusses a repeat purchase, but final Sold By is 3% Club; Customer Loyalty + Royalty Gift may still qualify.

## 114.10 Original sale participant may later earn Buying Commission
A participant in the original sale is not automatically barred from Buying Commission on later Buyback.

**Example:** Member earns Direct on original sale, later earns approved Buying Commission on Company Buyback, and may later earn Direct again on a genuine resale.

## 114.11 Reference Project steering by qualification timing
The first qualifying Reference event is decided by the qualifying milestone/tie-breaker even if payment timing influences which Project wins.

**Example:** Two candidate sales exist; the one reaching the qualifying milestone first consumes the lifetime Reference opportunity.

These accepted risks must not be silently changed by developers, staff or future reviewers without a new Owner-approved business-rule change.

- create Membership-required follow-up task;
- Customer may still purchase;
- repeat-purchase Loyalty remains separately eligible.

# 19. Repeat-Purchase Loyalty

Rules:

- first personal purchase = no repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- second and later personal Bookings may qualify immediately;
- no minimum 30-day gap;
- unlimited lifetime quantity;
- Project Loyalty must be Enabled;
- final Sold By must be 3% Club for Club-direct repeat-purchase treatment, unless another already-approved route applies;
- Member-closed repeat purchase does not generate Customer Loyalty;
- Deactivated Member cannot personally earn this Loyalty while deactivated.

Normal milestone = 100% Payment Received.

Buyback alternative requires source Booking >=25% Payment Received before Approved Buyback can qualify it.

- they cannot earn Customer-closing Loyalty;
- they cannot earn repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- existing protected transactions submitted while eligible retain frozen treatment.

Do not force self-purchase treatment merely because the Person previously had Active Member status.

# 25. Own-Sale Credit Generation

## 25.1 Unit based

Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may generate one Own-Sale Credit.

If one Booking contains multiple eligible units, create one credit per unit.

## 25.2 Eligible seller

Credit owner is the qualifying Member who genuinely owns the sale-side credit under the approved Sold By/self-purchase rule.

## 25.3 Anti-recycling uniqueness

# 28. Reference Credit — New Logic

No monetary Invite exists.

- no multi-level;
- no joining credit;
- each directly introduced Member can contribute one lifetime Reference Credit;
- self-purchase does not consume Reference opportunity;
- only first Reference-eligible third-party sale inside an active Trip Programme can consume it;
- sale in a Project with Trip Disabled does not consume it;
- multi-unit first qualifying transaction gives seller multiple Own-Sale Credits but inviter only one Reference Credit.

# 29. Reference Candidate and Atomic Consumption

Reference opportunity must be concurrency safe.

At qualification milestone:

1. verify introduced Member's lifetime Reference opportunity is still unused;
2. verify sale is third-party, Reference-eligible and Trip-enabled under frozen version;
3. acquire row lock / use atomic conditional update;
4. apply tie-breaker if competing qualification timestamps exist;
5. create exactly one Reference Credit;
6. mark opportunity consumed;
7. persist winning Booking/Project.

Tie-breaker:

1. earliest verified qualification timestamp;
2. if exact equal, lower permanent Booking Number.

Payment timing may influence which Project wins. Do not replace this with Booking Request order.

# 32. Trip Bucket Opening / Freeze

A bucket opens when:

> the Member receives the first valid Pending Trip Credit from an Accounts-approved Booking Request in that Project.

Freeze into the bucket:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- self-purchase rule snapshot;
- eligible inventory/programme identity;
- Trip Programme Version;
- Trip Terms Version.

If the credit that opened the bucket later reverses and no other valid Pending/Qualified credit remains, close that empty bucket. The next valid credit opens a new bucket under the then-current applicable Programme Version.

This prevents a throwaway/rejected transaction from permanently locking easier rules.

---

# 33. Trip Bucket Completion

A bucket is Earned when:

- total allocated Qualified credits >= Total Target;
- Own-Sale allocated count >= Minimum Own;
- Reference allocated count <= Maximum Reference;
- credits are not Expired/Reversed;
- credit fulfilment holds do not prevent reward fulfilment.

Reference is optional; Own-Sale may satisfy the whole target.

Use deterministic FIFO allocation.

---

# 34. FIFO Allocation

Allocation order:

- earliest Qualified eligible credits first;
- respect credit type constraints;
- respect Minimum Own / Maximum Reference;
- used credits cannot be reused.

Recommended approach:

- allocate Own credits needed to satisfy Minimum Own;
- fill remaining target using earliest eligible Own/Reference credits while never exceeding Max Reference;
- preserve deterministic ordering by `qualifiedAt`, then permanent ID.

Do not give Members manual cherry-picking rights unless future rule changes.

---

# 35. Banked Credits and Expiry

Qualified but unused Trip Credits:

- remain banked within the same Project/programme;
- expire 12 months after qualification;
- cannot move to another Project;
- cannot be reused after allocation to a fulfilled reward.

Scheduled expiry must be idempotent.

Already Earned Trip Rewards use Trip Terms redemption deadlines, not the generic 12-month credit-expiry rule.

- existing frozen eligible activity may continue according to disclosed closure rules;
- open bucket gets a final wind-down/completion deadline from Trip Terms;
- no silent retroactive deletion of valid progress.

Store closure/cut-off metadata in programme/version configuration or linked Terms reference.

# 82. Payment Received Event Processing Order

Recommended transaction order when Payment Received changes:

1. validate/correct Payment Received entry;
2. calculate cumulative verified percentage;
3. persist payment event;
4. evaluate Direct milestone;
5. evaluate Customer Loyalty milestone;
6. evaluate Own-Sale Trip qualification;
7. evaluate Reference candidate qualification;
8. evaluate Royalty relationship finalisation or Royalty reward qualification where relevant;
9. update tasks/holds;
10. write audit;
11. commit.

Each sub-rule must be idempotent.

Do not assume all rewards qualify at the same percentage.

---

# 83. Approved Buyback Event Processing Order

When Buyback becomes Approved:

1. load source Booking and verified Payment Received;
2. determine whether source payment >=25%;
3. if below 25%, mark reward alternative milestone not available;
4. if >=25%, re-evaluate Customer Loyalty;
5. re-evaluate Own-Sale Trip Credit;
6. re-evaluate Reference Credit;
7. re-evaluate Royalty relationship/reward eligibility;
8. do not accelerate Direct;
9. persist qualification route = Approved Buyback where used;
10. apply fulfilment hold pending Stable Completion where required;
11. create/update Buyback reward-review task;
12. audit.

---

# 84. Buyback Unwind Processing Order

When Approved Buyback is cancelled/unwound:

1. identify benefits/rewards qualified solely through that Buyback;
2. check whether normal 100% Payment Received milestone independently exists;
3. preserve independently valid entitlement;
4. reverse Buyback-only qualification where not independently valid;
5. recalculate Trip bucket;
6. reverse/unhold Royalty Credit as applicable;
7. create monetary Recovery/adjustment for paid invalid Loyalty if applicable;
8. never reopen an opportunity already consumed by a Travelled Trip or Delivered Gift;
9. audit.

# 3% CLUB REAL ESTATE
# Sales & Reference Trip Reward — Programme Terms

**Business Model:** v2  
**Version:** 1.0 Draft for Final Approval  
**Date:** 9 October 2026  
**Effective Date:** Only when the applicable Project Trip Programme Version is approved and activated  
**Timezone:** Asia/Kolkata

> These Terms govern the non-cash Sales & Reference Trip Reward and must be read with Business Model v2, the applicable Project Trip Programme Version, Member Terms and transaction records.

## 1. Purpose

The Trip Reward recognises genuine Project sales performance and limited first-sale contribution from directly introduced Members. It is not a joining reward, recruitment reward, multi-level reward or cash commission.

## 2. Project-specific programme

A Trip Reward exists only where the Project has **Trip Programme = Enabled**.

Each live Project Trip Programme must state:
- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- eligible/excluded inventory;
- Programme Version;
- Terms Version;
- package reference;
- cut-off/end date;
- final wind-down deadline;
- booking/redemption window.

## 3. Credit types

### Own-Sale Credit
Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may create one Own-Sale Credit.

A multi-unit Booking may create multiple Own-Sale Credits.

### Reference Credit
A directly introduced Member can contribute one lifetime Reference Credit to the immediate inviter.

Rules:
- immediate inviter only;
- no multi-level credit;
- no joining credit;
- self-purchase does not consume the Reference opportunity;
- a Trip-disabled Project does not consume it;
- first qualifying multi-unit referred sale creates only one Reference Credit.

## 4. Trip composition

Each Project defines:
- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits.

Reference Credits are optional. A Member may complete the whole target through Own-Sale Credits if the programme permits.

## 5. Normal qualification

Normal qualification milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Before qualification: **Pending Trip Credit**  
After qualification: **Qualified Trip Credit**

Pending Credit is not an Earned Trip Reward.

## 6. Same Member + same Unit anti-recycling

> **One Member may earn at most one Own-Sale Credit for the same Plot/Unit within the same Project Trip Programme across all sale types.**

This applies to self-purchase, third-party sale and resale after Buyback.

A different Member may later earn a genuine credit on that Unit.

## 7. Member self-purchase

Formal Member self-purchase may count toward Own-Sale Trip progress.

There is no general cap on genuine self-purchase Credits, but the same Member/unit/programme anti-recycling rule applies.

Self-purchase by an introduced Member does not create or consume the inviter's Reference opportunity.

## 8. Subdivision

If an eligible Unit is subdivided after programme activation, the child Units share one Credit pool from the parent Unit unless a new MD-approved Programme Version explicitly treats them separately.

## 9. Change Plot

The final valid Unit must be eligible under the Booking's frozen Trip Programme Version. If the final Unit is excluded, the pending/qualified Credit reverses as applicable.

## 10. Buyback alternative milestone

Approved Buyback can qualify Trip Credits only if the source Booking has already reached:

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

Buyback does not accelerate Direct Commission.

The Trip Credit remains attached to the source sale, not to the person arranging the Buyback.

## 11. Stable Buyback Completion

A Buyback-qualified Trip Credit cannot be used for Trip fulfilment until **Stable Buyback Completion**.

Stable Buyback Completion requires:
1. Buyback/Acquisition formally Approved;
2. Payment Given = 100% verified;
3. no cancellation/unwind;
4. applicable completion/document-return conditions completed, including where relevant Allotment papers or Registry-back requirements.

## 12. Buyback unwind

If Buyback-created qualification later unwinds before Stable Buyback Completion:
- Buyback-based qualification reverses;
- normal 100% Payment Received may still qualify independently;
- no duplicate Credit is created.

## 13. Reference qualification and tie-break

The one lifetime Reference opportunity is consumed by the first Reference-eligible third-party sale in an active Trip Programme that reaches the required milestone.

If two candidate sales qualify at exactly the same time:
1. earliest verified qualification timestamp wins;
2. if equal, lower permanent Booking Number wins.

## 14. Deactivated inviter

If the Reference event qualifies while the inviter is Deactivated:
- the lifetime opportunity is consumed;
- the Reference Credit is created;
- use/fulfilment is held until valid reactivation.

## 15. Bucket opening and freeze

A Trip bucket opens only when the eligible Booking Request version is approved by Accounts and creates the Member's first valid Pending Trip Credit for that Project.

At opening, freeze:
- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- self-purchase rules;
- eligible inventory;
- Programme Version;
- Terms Version.

Rejected/abandoned requests freeze nothing.

## 16. FIFO

Qualified Credits are allocated **earliest qualified first**, subject to:
- Minimum Own requirement;
- Maximum Reference allowance;
- Credit type;
- frozen programme rules.

Used Credits cannot be reused.

## 17. Banked Reference Credits

Reference Credits above the current bucket maximum remain banked for the same Project, subject to future bucket rules, FIFO and expiry.

## 18. Credit expiry

Unused Qualified Trip Credits expire:

> **12 months after qualification**

Earned Trip Rewards follow their separate booking/redemption window.

## 19. Programme closure

Project Trip Terms must state:
- programme cut-off;
- treatment of Pending Credits;
- treatment of Qualified Credits;
- final wind-down/completion deadline.

Protected pre-cut-off activity continues under its frozen version until the published final deadline.

## 20. When a Trip becomes Earned

A Trip Reward becomes Earned only when:
- Total Target is met;
- Minimum Own requirement is met;
- Reference Credits do not exceed the maximum;
- allocated Credits are valid and unexpired.

Earned does not mean Booked or Travelled.

## 21. Repeat Trips

Trip Rewards may repeat while the Programme remains active. Once a bucket is Earned, its used Credits are permanently allocated and cannot complete another Trip.

## 22. Reward owner and nominee

The earning Member remains the owner of the Reward.

Permitted nominee:
- immediate family; or
- one non-family nominee with MD approval.

Nominee identity must be recorded. Nomination does not transfer Credit ownership.

## 23. No automatic cash alternative

There is no automatic cash alternative for the Trip Reward.

## 24. Trip package

Before activation, the Programme Annexure must state:
- destination/package;
- duration;
- number of travellers covered;
- transport;
- accommodation;
- meals;
- local transfers;
- activities/sightseeing if included;
- booking window;
- travel window;
- exclusions;
- blackout/availability rules.

No specific destination/package should be publicly promised before approval.

## 25. Standard exclusions unless expressly included

Examples:
- passport;
- visa;
- insurance;
- personal transport outside package;
- meals not included;
- shopping;
- minibar;
- upgrades;
- excess baggage;
- optional activities;
- personal medical expenses.

## 26. Passport / visa

Traveller is responsible for passport and visa eligibility. Visa approval is not guaranteed. Visa denial does not create an automatic cash alternative.

## 27. Member cancellation / no-show

After booking, supplier cancellation/rebooking conditions apply.

A no-show may cause the Reward to be treated as consumed according to the published Programme Terms.

## 28. Reward reversal stages

**Before Earned:** invalid Credit reverses and progress recalculates.

**Earned but not Booked:** invalid used Credit may make the Reward Deficient; eligible unused Credit may backfill under FIFO.

**Booked but not Travelled:** published cancellation/rebooking rules apply.

**Travel Completed:** no ordinary business-event clawback.

## 29. Fulfilment finality

Once Travel is completed:

> all Credits/opportunities used for that Reward remain consumed forever.

Fraud, sham activity or material misrepresentation may still result in Recovery, discipline or other lawful action.

## 30. Recovery and deactivation

Recovery Outstanding or Member Deactivation:
- does not erase recorded progress/history;
- holds Trip fulfilment until the relevant block is cleared.

## 31. Staff conflicts

Staff/defined close-relative benefit requires conflict disclosure and MD approval. A staff member cannot approve their own entitlement.

## 32. Correction after fulfilment

A Sold By/beneficiary correction after Travel does not automatically create a second Trip. Any exceptional settlement requires separate MD approval outside the reward-credit engine.

## 33. Fraud

No Reward is valid for fake Customers, sham Bookings, false Sold By, circular reward farming, duplicate identity abuse or deliberate same-Unit recycling.

## 34. Tax / statutory treatment

Applicable tax, withholding and reporting treatment follows law and professional advice. Do not advertise the Reward as tax-free unless professionally confirmed.

## 35. CRM record

CRM should record/reference:

- Earned date;

- not passive income;
- not multi-level;

## 2. No monetary Royalty

## 3. Relationship creation

**Sold By Customer / Sold By 3% Club:** no Member Royalty relationship is created.

A later Member sale does not retroactively create the first-purchase relationship.

## 4. Primary Customer only

Only the Primary Customer establishes the Royalty relationship. Additional Customers do not each create separate Royalty relationships.

## 5. Relationship finalisation

- 100% verified Payment Received; or

## 7. Cancellation before finalisation

- provisional relationship is removed from current entitlement;

Each qualifying Primary Customer creates one Royalty opportunity for life for the valid Royalty Linked Member.

Background/informal involvement by the Royalty Linked Member does not automatically reclassify the sale.

Fraudulent data entry remains prohibited.

One qualifying event creates one Royalty Credit, which creates one Gift entitlement under the frozen Programme Version.

Credits do not combine into points unless a future approved Programme explicitly introduces that feature.
