# 5. MASTER OLD-LOGIC REMOVAL MATRIX

## OL-01 — Universal Fixed 3% Direct Commission

**Old logic:**  
Direct Commission is treated as a fixed 3% business-wide rate.

**Found in old material:**  
- Complete Business Model Master
- Commission Business Decision Rulebook
- Dashboard Content Pack
- older pitch/FAQ wording

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**  
Direct is Project-specific.

- Project may enable or disable Direct.
- Project Direct hard maximum = 5%.
- Applicable rate freezes on the Booking Request version Accounts ultimately approves.
- “3% Club” remains the brand name, not a universal rate promise.

**Remove from live implementation:**

- hard-coded `3%` Direct assumption;
- calculator defaults that treat 3% as universal;
- portal copy saying every eligible sale earns 3%;
- dashboard text saying “3% Direct Commission” as a system-wide rule;
- MIS/report labels implying Direct is always 3%;
- validation that expects Direct rate to equal 3%.

**Replacement required:**  
Project Settings → Direct Enabled/Disabled + Direct Rate + Version ID.

---

## OL-02 — Monetary Member Invite Commission

**Old logic:**  
Immediate inviter receives a monetary commission on the directly invited Member's first qualifying third-party sale.

**Status:** **REMOVE / REPLACE**

**New v2 replacement:**  
**Reference Credit** inside the Sales & Reference Trip Reward.

New Reference logic is non-cash and:

- immediate inviter only;
- no joining reward;
- no multi-level reward;
- one directly introduced Member can contribute one lifetime Reference Credit;
- self-purchase does not consume the Reference opportunity;
- first Reference-eligible third-party sale in an active Trip Programme consumes it;
- one qualifying multi-unit first sale still creates only one Reference Credit;
- Reference Credit is Project-locked when qualified;
- Reference Credits participate in Trip target composition.

**Remove from live implementation:**

- Invite Commission monetary calculation;
- Invite Commission payable amount;
- Invite Commission payout records;
- Invite Commission Ready/Paid states as a cash component;
- Invite percentage rate;
- Invite percentage lookup;
- Invite commission ledger entries;
- Invite cash payout export columns;
- Invite cash tax/payout logic;
- Invite-specific commission calculator output.

**Keep:**  
The Membership inviter relationship itself remains required for Reference logic.

## OL-15 — Enquiry-Based “Original Introduced By” as Royalty Source

**Old logic:**

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**

- Introduced Customer position assignment from Enquiry;

**Keep:**

- Gift fulfilment waits for underlying 100% or Stable Buyback Completion;
- once Gift is delivered, opportunity remains consumed forever;
- fraud/recovery can still be pursued without creating a second Gift.

# 7. CUSTOMER LOYALTY OLD LOGIC TO REMOVE OR REPLACE

## OL-17 — Old Flat 1% Loyalty Assumption

**Old logic:**  
Customer Loyalty rate = fixed 1%.

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**  
Customer Loyalty is Project-specific.

- Enabled/Disabled per Project.
- Hard maximum = 3%.
- Normally lower than Direct.
- Exceptional economics require MD reason where Direct Disabled or Loyalty >= Direct.
- Frozen on approved Booking Request version.

**Remove:**

- hard-coded 1%;
- calculator assumption of 1%;
- dashboard/portal copy stating 1% universally.

---

## OL-18 — Combined Lifetime Maximum of 3 Loyalty Benefits

**Old logic:**  
A Customer may receive only 3 Loyalty benefits total across:

- Customer-closing; and
- repeat personal purchase.

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**

### Customer-closing route
Maximum:

> **3 successful Customer-closing Loyalty events for life**

After that:

> Active Membership required for future third-party selling earnings.

### Repeat-purchase route
> **Unlimited**

Therefore a Customer who has used all 3 Customer-closing events may still receive eligible repeat-purchase Loyalty.

---

## OL-19 — “Fourth Loyalty Always Blocked”

**Old logic:**  
System prevents any fourth Loyalty benefit.

**Status:** **REMOVE**

**New v2 rule:**  
Only a fourth **Customer-closing** Loyalty is blocked.

A fourth, fifth or later **repeat-purchase** Loyalty may be valid.

**Remove/rewrite:**

- `Loyalty 1/3, 2/3, 3/3` as one universal counter;
- global `Loyalty exhausted`;
- universal fourth-Loyalty rejection;
- tests asserting no Loyalty after three regardless of route.

---

## OL-20 — Old Membership Invitation Trigger Wording

**Old logic:**  
Third lifetime Loyalty exhausts all Loyalty and triggers Membership invitation.

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**  
Third **Customer-closing** Loyalty triggers the requirement to become Active Member for future third-party selling earnings.

Repeat-purchase Loyalty remains available.

**Dashboard/task wording must change from:**

> `Loyalty 3/3 used — No further Sold By Customer activity`

to wording that clearly says:

> `Customer-closing Loyalty limit reached — Membership required for future third-party selling. Repeat-purchase Loyalty remains separately eligible.`

---

## OL-21 — Old Deactivated Member Loyalty Logic

**Old logic:**  
Older material mainly blocks new Customer-closing Loyalty while Deactivated.

**Status:** **REPLACE**

**New v2 rule:**  
While Deactivated, the Person cannot personally earn either:

- Customer-closing Loyalty;
- repeat-purchase Loyalty.

But:

- they may buy as a normal Customer;
- another genuine Member may earn Direct on that sale under normal rules;
- normal Royalty relationship rules may apply.

# 8. OLD 4% CAP LOGIC TO REMOVE

- Accounts approval block based solely on >4%;
- auto/conflict logic based on total percentage.

# 9. OLD BUYBACK REWARD LOGIC TO REPLACE

## OL-24 — Buyback Accelerates Invite/Royalty/Loyalty Without Minimum Source Payment

> **at least 25% verified Payment Received**

This applies to:

- Customer Loyalty;
- Own-Sale Trip Credit;
- Reference Credit;
- Royalty Reward eligibility.

Buyback does **not** accelerate Direct.
