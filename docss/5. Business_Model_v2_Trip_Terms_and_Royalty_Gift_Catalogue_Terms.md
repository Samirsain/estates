# PART I — COMMON REWARD TERMS

# 1. Purpose

These Terms govern the non-cash reward programmes offered by 3% Club:

1. **Sales & Reference Trip Reward**
2. **Royalty Relationship Reward**

They explain:

- how rewards become eligible;
- when rewards can actually be fulfilled;
- programme versioning;
- nominees/recipients;
- cancellation and reversal;
- Recovery and deactivation holds;
- Buyback-related qualification;
- redemption and delivery;
- tax/statutory treatment;
- programme closure.

These Terms do not create any reward merely because a person joins 3% Club.

---

# 2. Governing Business Model

These Terms must be read with:

- Business Model v2;
- applicable Project Settings Version;
- applicable Trip Programme Version;
- the Royalty token rate saved on the qualifying Booking Request version (docss/6.2 v2.0);
- Member Terms;
- applicable Booking/Buyback documents.

If a Project-specific schedule conflicts with Business Model v2:

> **Business Model v2 controls unless a later Owner-approved business-rule change expressly supersedes it.**

---

# 3. No Guaranteed Reward

A Member is not guaranteed:

- a Trip;
- Royalty Tokens;
- a particular destination;
- a particular brand/model;
- a particular travel date;
- a cash amount;
- a resale value;
- an exchange value.

A reward arises only when the applicable qualification rules are satisfied.

---

# 4. No Automatic Cash Alternative

Trip Rewards and Royalty Tokens are:

> **non-cash rewards.**

There is no automatic right to:

- cash instead of a Trip;
- cash instead of Royalty Tokens;
- cash for an unused Trip;
- cash for unused or unavailable Royalty Tokens;
- cash for a nominee change;
- cash because the Member does not wish to travel.

Any exceptional commercial settlement requires separate written approval and does not alter the reward-credit rules.

---

# 5. Reward Ownership

The earning Member remains the owner of the reward entitlement.

Underlying:

- Own-Sale Credits;
- Reference Credits;
- Trip Reward;
- Royalty Credit

do not transfer ownership to another person merely because a nominee or recipient is selected.

---

# 6. Nominee / Recipient Rule

A reward may be fulfilled in favour of:

1. the earning Member;
2. an **immediate-family nominee**; or
3. **one non-family nominee** (no MD approval needed).

Nominee/recipient identity must be recorded before fulfilment.

A non-family nominee needs no MD approval (owner ruling, 14 Oct 2026).

The reward cannot be openly traded or sold through the CRM reward process.

---

# 7. Immediate Family

For these reward Terms, immediate family should be recorded using the Company-approved relationship categories.

Recommended operating categories:

- spouse;
- parent;
- child;
- sibling.

**Final legal definition:** [TO BE APPROVED IN MEMBER TERMS / LEGAL REVIEW]

---

# 8. Recovery Hold

If the earning Member has **Recovery Outstanding**:

- reward progress remains recorded;
- earned entitlement remains recorded;
- physical/non-cash fulfilment is held;
- the reward may proceed only after Recovery is Cleared, unless a separately authorised exception exists under Company policy.

A hold does not itself delete the entitlement.

---

# 9. Member Deactivation Hold

If the earning Member is Deactivated:

- earned reward history remains;
- eligible reward history remains;
- fulfilment is held;
- fulfilment may resume after valid reactivation if all other conditions remain satisfied.

No new entitlement is created merely because the Member is later reactivated.

---

# 10. Staff / Related-Staff Conflict

Where the earning Member/recipient falls under the approved staff or staff-relative conflict rules:

- fulfilment is held until required conflict approval is completed;
- staff cannot self-approve their own reward;
- audit history must be retained.

---

# 11. Fraud / Sham / Misrepresentation

No reward is protected by:

- fake Booking;
- fake Customer;
- false Sold By;
- duplicate identity abuse;
- collusive transaction;
- circular reward farming;
- artificial transaction;
- material misrepresentation;
- forged documents.

The Company may:

- hold fulfilment;
- reverse an unfulfilled reward;
- create Recovery where applicable;
- take Membership/disciplinary action.

A fulfilled reward remains consumed for reward-opportunity purposes even where separate fraud recovery/action is pursued.

---

# 12. Versioning

Each live reward programme must have a dated/versioned reference.

A new version is required for material changes such as:

- Trip target;
- Trip package;
- destination/type;
- eligible inventory;
- Min Own / Max Reference composition;
- redemption deadlines;
- Royalty Token Terms and each Project's token rate;
- substitution rules;
- fulfilment rules.

Approved versions must not be silently overwritten.

---

# 13. No Backdating

A Programme Version cannot become effective earlier than its approval time.

Applicable programme/version references must be stored on the relevant CRM record.

---

# 14. Communication

Only approved:

- Programme Terms;
- Project Settings;
- Trip schedule;
- Royalty Token Terms;
- official Company communications

may be used to promise reward details.

Members must not independently promise:

- a destination;
- a hotel;
- a flight;
- a phone model;
- a gold weight;
- what Royalty Tokens will be redeemed for, or when;
- a cash alternative

unless it appears in an approved active programme.

# 18. Own-Sale Credit

Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may create:

> **one Own-Sale Credit**

for the qualifying Member.

A multi-unit Booking may create multiple Own-Sale Credits.

---

# 19. Same Member / Same Unit Anti-Recycling

One Member may receive at most:

> **one Own-Sale Credit for the same Plot/Unit in the same Project Trip Programme**

across:

- self-purchase;
- third-party sale;
- resale after Buyback.

A different Member may later earn a genuine credit on that same unit if all rules otherwise qualify.

---

# 20. Member Self-Purchase

A formal Member self-purchase may create Own-Sale Credit.

There is no general cap on genuine self-purchased units counting toward Own-Sale requirements.

The same Member/unit/programme anti-recycling rule still applies.

---

# 21. Reference Credit

A Reference Credit is available only to the **immediate Membership inviter**.

Rules:

- no joining credit;
- no multi-level credit;
- one directly introduced Member may contribute only one lifetime Reference Credit;
- self-purchase by that introduced Member does not consume the Reference opportunity;
- a sale in a Project without an active eligible Trip Programme does not consume the opportunity;
- the qualifying third-party sale consumes the lifetime Reference opportunity.

---

# 22. Multi-Unit First Referred Sale

If the introduced Member's first eligible referred transaction contains multiple units:

- selling Member may receive multiple Own-Sale Credits;
- immediate inviter may receive only **one Reference Credit**.

---

# 23. Trip Credit States

CRM may use states including:

- Pending;
- Qualified;
- Allocated;
- Used;
- Held;
- Reversed;
- Expired.

These states do not create a cash value.

---

# 24. Normal Qualification

Normal Trip Credit qualification occurs at:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Before that, an eligible approved Booking may create Pending Credit under the frozen Programme rules.

---

# 25. Approved Buyback Alternative

Approved Buyback may qualify a Trip Credit before source Payment Received reaches 100% only when:

> **source Booking has reached at least 25% verified Payment Received.**

If source Payment Received is below 25%:

> Buyback does not qualify the Trip Credit.

---

# 26. Buyback Does Not Accelerate Direct

Trip qualification through Buyback does not change the Direct Commission milestone.

Direct continues under its own Business Model rules.

---

# 27. Stable Buyback Completion

A Buyback-qualified Trip Credit may be Qualified before physical fulfilment.

Actual Trip booking/fulfilment waits until **Stable Buyback Completion**.

Stable Buyback Completion requires all applicable conditions:

1. Buyback/Acquisition formally Approved;
2. Payment Given = 100% verified;
3. Buyback not cancelled/unwound;
4. applicable acquisition/document-return requirements completed, including where relevant:
   - Allotment papers returned;
   - Registry-back completed;
   - other configured acquisition-completion conditions.

---

# 28. Buyback Unwind

If Buyback qualification later unwinds before Stable Completion:

- Buyback-only qualification reverses;
- normal source-sale qualification may still apply if independently achieved;
- no duplicate credit is created.

---

# 29. Trip Bucket

A Trip bucket opens when:

> the eligible Booking Request version is approved by Accounts and creates the Member's first valid Pending Trip Credit for that Project/programme.

The bucket freezes:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- self-purchase rules;
- eligible inventory;
- Trip Programme Version;
- Trip Terms Version.

---

# 30. Composition Rule

To earn the Trip Reward, the Member must satisfy both:

1. Total Target; and
2. Minimum Own-Sale requirement.

Reference Credits may contribute only up to the Maximum Reference allowance.

Example:

> Target 9 / Minimum Own 6 / Maximum Reference 3.

Then 6 Own + 3 Reference qualifies, while 5 Own + 4 Reference does not.

---

# 31. Reference Credits Are Optional

A Member may satisfy the entire Trip Target using Own-Sale Credits.

Reference Credits are not mandatory.

---

# 32. Extra Reference Credits

Qualified Reference Credits above the current bucket's permitted Reference allowance remain:

> **banked in the same Project**

subject to:

- FIFO;
- expiry;
- later bucket composition.

They do not move to another Project.

---

# 33. FIFO

Credits are used:

> **earliest qualified eligible credits first**

subject to:

- credit type;
- Minimum Own requirement;
- Maximum Reference allowance;
- frozen programme rules.

A credit used for one completed Trip cannot be reused.

---

# 34. Credit Expiry

Unused Qualified Trip Credits expire:

> **12 months after qualification.**

Expiry applies to unused Qualified Credits.

Already Earned Trip Rewards follow the Trip Reward redemption deadline in the applicable Trip Schedule.

---

# 35. Programme Closure

When a Trip Programme closes:

- no post-cut-off approved Booking may create new credit under that programme;
- protected pre-cut-off approved activity may continue under its frozen terms;
- open buckets must be completed by the published final wind-down deadline.

**Final wind-down deadline:** [TO BE APPROVED PER PROGRAMME]

No valid progress is silently deleted before the disclosed deadline.

---

# 36. Earning the Reward

When the bucket satisfies its frozen target/composition:

> Trip Reward becomes **Earned**

subject to applicable holds.

Credits used for the Earned reward are allocated/consumed under the system rules.

---

# 37. Repeatable Reward

After a bucket is validly completed:

- used credits cannot be reused;
- a fresh bucket may begin;
- only fresh/unused valid credits may satisfy the next reward.

Repeatability exists only while the applicable Project Programme/Terms allow it.

---

2. On Hold, if applicable
3. Booking Requested

# PART III — ROYALTY RELATIONSHIP REWARD TERMS

# 57. Nature of the Royalty Relationship Reward

Royalty Relationship Reward is:

> **a one-time non-cash relationship reward**

linked to a qualifying long-term Customer relationship.

It is not:

- a percentage commission;
- a cash Royalty;
- an annual cycle;
- a position/band system;
- a multi-level reward.

---

# 58. Royalty Relationship Source

Only the **Primary Customer** can create the relationship from a Booking.

At the Primary Customer's earliest qualifying first purchase:

### Sold By Member
The selling Member may become the Provisional Royalty Linked Member.

### Sold By Customer / Sold By 3% Club
No Member Royalty relationship is created.

---

# 59. Relationship Finalisation

The Royalty relationship becomes Final when the first qualifying purchase reaches:

1. 100% verified Payment Received; or
2. qualifying Approved Buyback.

For Buyback to qualify:

> source Booking must already have at least 25% verified Payment Received.

---

# 60. Cancellation Before Finalisation

If the first purchase cancels before:

- 100% Payment Received; and
- any qualifying Approved Buyback,

the Provisional relationship does not remain a current entitlement.

A later genuine qualifying first purchase may establish the appropriate relationship.

---

# 61. One Opportunity Per Primary Customer

Each qualifying Primary Customer may create:

> **one lifetime Royalty opportunity**

for the valid Royalty Linked Member.

---

# 62. Reward-Triggering Future Purchase

The Royalty Reward may arise on the Primary Customer's first qualifying future:

> **direct personal purchase through 3% Club**

while the Royalty opportunity remains unused.

---

# 63. Sold By Member on Future Purchase

If another Member is final Sold By on the reward-triggering repeat purchase:

- no Royalty Credit is created on that Booking;
- the unused Royalty opportunity remains available.

---

# 64. Final Sold By Controls Club-Direct Classification

The final approved Sold By selection controls whether a repeat purchase is treated as:

> Sold By 3% Club

for Royalty Reward purposes.

Background/informal Member involvement does not automatically reclassify the transaction.

---

# 65–87. Royalty Tokens (replaces the Royalty Gift sections)

The Royalty Relationship Reward is paid in Royalty Tokens under docss/6.2 v2.0 (Royalty Token Terms), which governs:

- the per-Project token rate, frozen on each Booking Request version and used from the triggering purchase's Project;
- pending, posted and restricted tokens, including the Buyback gate (source 100% or Stable Buyback Completion);
- one pooled balance per Member across all Projects; no expiry, no cash value, no transfer;
- redemption arranged outside the CRM and recorded in it, for the Member, immediate family or a non-family recipient, with no MD approval;
- reversals exactly once, negative balances as non-cash deficits, and permanent consumption of a used award.

No Gift catalogue, Programme Version, selection, ordering or delivery applies.

- reason;
- source transaction;
- actor/system event;
- date/time;
- previous state;
- new state.

---

- Trip Travelled; or
- Royalty tokens of the event were used,

the fulfilled reward remains consumed for that event.

No automatic second Trip or Royalty award is created.

Any exceptional compensation requires separate MD settlement outside the reward engine.

## Common

# 103. Final Principle

> **A reward is earned under the frozen Business Model and Programme rules, but it is fulfilled only under the approved Trip Terms or Royalty Token Terms applicable to that entitlement. Programme changes must be prospective, fulfilment must be auditable, and no non-cash reward carries an automatic cash alternative.**
