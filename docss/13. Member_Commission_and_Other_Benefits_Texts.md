# Member Profile — Commission and Other Benefits Columns

As of 17/10/2026 · from the CRM code and the live database

What each of the two columns in **Bookings & Benefits** can show, what every text means, and whether it is in the data today.

---

## 1. Commission column

Each commission line on the row prints as two lines, sometimes three:

```
Direct 3.00% at 25% paid          ← line 1: type · rate · milestone
Ready · Paid 10/08/2026           ← line 2: eligibility · payment · date
Recovery outstanding              ← line 3: hold reason (amber), only when on hold
```

A row with no commission shows **—**. A row with two lines (for example Direct on the Booking and Buying from its Buyback) shows both, one under the other.

### Line 1 — type, rate, milestone

| Text | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Direct** | The Member sold this Plot. Paid to the selling Member. | Yes |
| **Loyalty** | Paid to the Customer who brought the buyer, or to a repeat buyer. Usually shows under *Commission from other Bookings*, not on the Member's own rows. | Yes |
| **Buying** | The Member arranged a Buyback or Purchase for Resale. Shows on the Buyback row. | Yes |
| **3.00%** | The commission rate, from the Booking's frozen commission terms. Seen today: Direct 2%, 3%, 3.5%; Loyalty 1%; Buying 1% (capped at 5%). | Yes |
| **at 25% paid** | Becomes due once the Customer has paid 25%. The normal Direct milestone. | Yes |
| **at 100% paid** | Becomes due only at full payment. Used for Loyalty, Buying (100% Payment Given), and Direct when the Member buys for themselves. | Yes |

### Line 2 — eligibility

| Text | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Milestone Pending** | The Customer has not yet paid the milestone %. Not due yet. | Yes |
| **Ready** | Milestone reached and nothing blocks it. Accounts can pay it. | Yes |
| **On Hold** | Something blocks it. The reason shows on line 3. | Yes |

### Line 2 — payment

| Text | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Not Paid** | Not paid yet. | Yes |
| **Paid 10/08/2026** | Paid on that date. | Yes |
| **Paid Early 10/08/2026** | Paid before it became Ready. Accounts must give a remark. | Yes |
| **Paid, needs fixing** | The money was already paid, but later the deal changed: the Booking was cancelled, the seller was corrected, or the Plot was changed. Accounts must fix the amount or take the money back. | Yes |
| **Cancelled** | The deal was undone before payment. Nothing is payable. | Yes |
| **· Not valid** | An old line that a newer line has replaced, for example after the seller of the Booking was corrected. You see it only when the second **All** filter is on. | Yes |

### How eligibility and payment combine

| Combination | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Milestone Pending · Not Paid | Waiting for the Customer's payment. | Yes |
| Milestone Pending · Paid Early | Paid before the milestone was reached. | Yes |
| Milestone Pending · Cancelled | The deal ended before it became due. | Yes |
| Ready · Not Paid | Due now. Accounts can pay. | Yes |
| Ready · Paid | Done. | Yes |
| Ready · Paid, needs fixing | Paid, then the deal was cancelled or changed. | Yes |
| On Hold · Not Paid | Due or nearly due, but blocked. | Yes |
| On Hold · Paid | Already paid, but now held, usually because of a Recovery. New payouts wait. | Yes |
| On Hold · Paid, needs fixing | Paid, needs adjustment, and is also blocked. | Yes |
| On Hold · Cancelled | Ended while it was on hold. | Yes |
| Ready · Paid Early, On Hold · Paid Early, Ready · Cancelled | Possible by the rules, not in the data yet. | No |

### Line 3 — hold reason (only with On Hold)

| Text on screen | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aadhaar pending** | The beneficiary's Aadhaar is not verified. | Yes |
| **Bank verification pending** | The bank account is not verified. | Yes |
| **Rera pending** | The Member's RERA registration is pending. | No |
| **Rera expired** | The Member's RERA has expired. | No |
| **Member commission hold** | MD or Admin put a Commission Hold on the Member. | No |
| **Member deactivated** | The Member is deactivated. | No |
| **Refund pending** | The Booking is being cancelled and a refund is pending. | Yes |
| **Change plot pending** | A Change Plot request is in progress on this Booking. | Yes |
| **Buyback pending** | A Buyback of this Booking is in progress. | Yes |
| **Payment pending** | The Customer's payment instalment is overdue. | No |
| **Closer kyc pending** | The Customer who closed the sale has no verified Aadhaar yet. | No |
| **Customer terms pending** | The Customer who closed the sale has not accepted Customer Terms. | No |
| **Recovery outstanding** | The beneficiary owes a Recovery. New cash payout waits until it is cleared. | Yes |
| **Old recovery pending** | After a beneficiary correction, the old beneficiary's paid amount is still to be recovered, unless MD approves. | Yes |
| **Staff conflict review** | The beneficiary is staff or a declared close relative. Waits for MD approval. | No |
| **Linked to recovery pending** | This person has the same mobile, bank account or ID as someone who still owes money back. Payment waits until Accounts or MD check it. | Yes |

## 2. Other benefits column

Benefits that came from **this exact Booking**, other than commission. A row with none shows **—**.

### Trip credit — `Trip · <state>`

One credit for each qualifying Booking the Member sold. Credits add up to a Trip, for example 3 credits make 1 Trip.

| Text | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Trip · Waiting for payment** | The credit is earned, but the Customer has not paid enough yet. It does not count yet. | Yes |
| **Trip · Counted for reward** | The credit counts towards a Trip reward. It covers three stages: counted for the next Trip, used in a Trip the Member won, and Trip travelled. Rewards (Trip) shows which. | Yes |
| **Trip · On hold** | Stopped for now, for example because the Member owes money back or is deactivated. | No |
| **Trip · Cancelled** | Taken back because the Booking was cancelled. | Yes |
| **Trip · Expired** | Not used in time, so it ran out. | No |

A **Reference Trip credit** (from an invited Member's Booking) belongs to that Member's Booking, not this one. It shows in Indirect Benefits → Rewards, not in this column.

### Royalty Points — `<n> Royalty Points · <state>`

Shows only when this Booking triggered a Royalty award to the Member, which happens when a Royalty linked Customer completes a purchase.

| Text | Meaning | In data now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **10 Royalty Points · Pending** | Awarded, waiting before it is added to the balance. | No |
| **10 Royalty Points · Posted** | Added to the Member's points balance. | Yes (10 and 15 points, not on a Member-sold row) |
| **10 Royalty Points · Reversed** | Taken back, for example after a cancellation. | No |

## 3. Quick reference

| Column | Can show |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | — · or one or more lines of *Type rate at milestone* / *Eligibility · Payment [date] [· Not valid]* / *[hold reason]* |
| Other benefits | — · *Trip · state* · *n Royalty Points · state* |
