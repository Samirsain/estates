# 7. Project Settings — New Required Model

Each Project must have versioned commercial/reward settings.

## 7.1 Required Project Settings Version fields

At minimum:

- `id`
- `projectId`
- `versionNumber` or immutable version identifier
- `status`: Draft / Approved / Active / Superseded
- `approvedByUserId`
- `approvedAt`
- `effectiveAt`
- `reason`
- `directEnabled`
- `directRatePct` nullable when Disabled
- `loyaltyEnabled`
- `loyaltyRatePct` nullable when Disabled
- `tripProgrammeEnabled`
- `tripTotalTarget` nullable when Disabled
- `tripMinOwnCredits` nullable when Disabled
- `tripMaxReferenceCredits` nullable when Disabled
- `tripTermsVersionRef`
- `economicsReviewed`
- `economicsReviewedBy`
- `economicsReviewedAt`

Eligible inventory may be represented through version-linked rows rather than a large payload field.

## 7.2 Validation

Hard validations:

- Direct rate <= 5%.
- Loyalty rate <= 3%.
- Disabled benefit must not silently behave as 0% enabled.
- Effective time cannot be earlier than approval time.
- Trip target > 0 when Trip Enabled.
- Minimum Own >= 0.
- Maximum Reference >= 0.
- `tripMinOwnCredits + tripMaxReferenceCredits` may be greater than target only if validation still enforces the target composition; recommended hard rule is `minOwn <= totalTarget` and `maxReference <= totalTarget`.
- no in-place editing of Approved/Active versions.

## 7.3 MD exception for abnormal Direct/Loyalty combination

If:

- Direct Disabled + Loyalty Enabled; or
- Loyalty rate >= Direct rate,

require:

- `commercialExceptionRequired = true`;
- MD approval;
- compulsory reason;
- audit event.

Do not hard-block a configuration after valid MD exception.

## 7.4 Economics review

Before Trip/Royalty-related Project activation where economics review is required, CRM stores only:

- Economics Reviewed = Yes/No;
- approver;
- date/time;
- Programme Version.

Do not store revenue, margin, Trip cost or Gift cost.

# 11. Final Freeze Point

The system must freeze rules from:

> **the Booking Request version that Accounts ultimately approves.**

Implementation consequences:

- first draft submission does not permanently freeze;
- rejected version freezes nothing;
- materially edited request creates a new immutable review version;
- final approved review version owns the frozen commercial/reward snapshot;
- no later lookup of “what rate was active on that date” may replace the stored frozen Version ID.

All calculators/recalculations for an existing Booking must use its frozen Version ID.

# 12. Sold By Classification

Retain final values:

- MEMBER
- CUSTOMER
- CLUB

Rules:

- one final Sold By classification per sale;
- Direct and Customer Loyalty cannot both be generated for the same sale;
- final Sold By selection controls Club-direct repeat-purchase treatment;
- background Member involvement does not automatically override final Sold By;
- Active Member cannot act as Sold By Customer;
- Deactivated Member may buy as Customer but cannot earn Customer Loyalty while deactivated.

Do not introduce automatic “material sourcing” reclassification logic.

# 13. Formal Member Self-Purchase

Self-purchase is true only when:

> Primary Customer is an Active Member.

Do not automatically treat a Booking as self-purchase because:

- Additional Customer is an Active Member;
- buyer is a spouse/relative of selling Member;
- buyer is otherwise associated with selling Member.

For formal self-purchase:

- buyer-Member gets Direct if Direct Enabled;
- Direct milestone = 100% Payment Received;
- no second selling Member Direct;
- no Customer Loyalty;
- self-purchase may generate Own-Sale Trip Credit;
- inviter gets no Reference Credit from the self-purchase;
- Buyback does not accelerate Direct.

This accepted-risk rule must be implemented exactly and must not be “tightened” by hidden family logic.

# 14. Direct Commission Engine

## 14.1 Rate

Use frozen Project Direct rate.

No hard-coded 3%.

## 14.2 Eligibility milestones

### Third-party Member sale

- Direct Enabled = true
- final Sold By = Member
- selling Member eligible
- milestone = 25% verified Payment Received

At 25%:

> full Direct percentage entitlement becomes Ready, subject to holds.

### Formal Member self-purchase

- Primary Customer is Active Member
- Direct Enabled = true
- milestone = 100% verified Payment Received

## 14.3 Buyback

Buyback does not accelerate Direct.

## 14.4 Cancellation/payment correction

If progress falls below milestone or sale becomes invalid:

- Not Paid → Milestone Pending/Cancelled as applicable;
- Paid/Paid Early → Accounts Adjustment Required / Recovery path;
- never delete the record.

## 14.5 Direct record snapshot

Store at minimum:

- Booking
- beneficiary Person/Member
- frozen Project Settings Version
- frozen rate percentage
- milestone type
- current eligibility state
- payment state
- hold reason
- supersession fields
- external processing reference/date where paid

- Recovery creation when later invalid;
- set-off against future monetary benefits for same Person;
- third-party payer reference capture where payment source differs from named buyer if current payment workflow supports payer metadata;
- audit of source payment corrections;
- Recovery hold on new cash payouts where applicable.

Do not split Direct into 50/50 stages.

1. `CUSTOMER_CLOSING`

# 17. Customer-Closer Eligibility

Before a Person may be final Sold By Customer, system must validate:

- Person has an approved personal purchase history;
- verified KYC is present under the approved Customer-closer rule;
- accepted Customer Terms/version exists;
- Person is not an Active Member;
- Person has fewer than 3 consumed successful Customer-closing Loyalty events.

The closer may still be:

- related to buyer;
- an Additional Customer on the Booking.

Do not add an automatic related-party prohibition.

---

# 18. Customer-Closing Loyalty

## 18.1 Rate

Use frozen Project Loyalty rate.

No fixed 1%.

## 18.2 Milestone

Normal:

> 100% verified Payment Received.

Alternative:

> qualifying Approved Buyback after source Booking had already reached at least 25% verified Payment Received.

## 18.3 Lifetime limit

Maximum:

> 3 successful Customer-closing Loyalty events for life.

Use atomic allocation.

Recommended logical event fields:

- `personId`
- `bookingId`
- `route = CUSTOMER_CLOSING`
- `qualifiedAt`
- `consumptionState`
- `cancelled/reversedAt`
- `fulfilled/paidState`

The count must be derived from current valid consumed events, not an editable integer alone.

## 18.4 After third event

- no fourth Customer-closing Loyalty;
- create Membership-required follow-up task;
- Customer may still purchase;
- repeat-purchase Loyalty remains separately eligible.

---

# 19. Repeat-Purchase Loyalty

Rules:

- first personal purchase = no repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- second and later personal Bookings may qualify immediately;
- no minimum 30-day gap;
- unlimited lifetime quantity;
- Project Loyalty must be Enabled;
- final Sold By must be 3% Club for Club-direct repeat-purchase treatment, unless another already-approved route applies;
- Member-closed repeat purchase does not generate Customer Loyalty;
- Deactivated Member cannot personally earn this Loyalty while deactivated.

Normal milestone = 100% Payment Received.

Buyback alternative requires source Booking >=25% Payment Received before Approved Buyback can qualify it.

# 20. Deactivated Member Logic

When Person has Member capability but status = Deactivated:

- they may be Primary Customer as a normal Customer;
- another Member may sell to them and earn normal eligible Direct;
- purchase may participate in normal Royalty relationship logic;
- they cannot earn Customer-closing Loyalty;
- they cannot earn repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- existing protected transactions submitted while eligible retain frozen treatment.

Do not force self-purchase treatment merely because the Person previously had Active Member status.

# 25. Own-Sale Credit Generation

## 25.1 Unit based

Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit may generate one Own-Sale Credit.

If one Booking contains multiple eligible units, create one credit per unit.

## 25.2 Eligible seller

Credit owner is the qualifying Member who genuinely owns the sale-side credit under the approved Sold By/self-purchase rule.

## 25.3 Anti-recycling uniqueness

Hard business rule:

> one Member may earn at most one Own-Sale Credit for the same Plot/Unit in the same Project Trip Programme across all sale types.

Recommended unique constraint/key:

`memberId + sourcePlotId + programmeIdentity`

where programme identity spans all versions belonging to the same Project Trip Programme, not merely the version row.

Do not let a new settings version reset this anti-recycling rule.

A different Member may later earn a genuine credit for the same unit.

---

# 26. Pending Own-Sale Credit

Create Pending Trip Credit only when:

- Booking Request version is approved by Accounts;
- Trip Programme was Enabled in the frozen version;
- final unit is eligible under frozen inventory rules;
- Member is eligible for Own-Sale Credit;
- anti-recycling uniqueness permits it.

This Pending event may open the Member's Trip bucket.

Rejected/raw requests create no Pending credit and open no bucket.

---

# 27. Own-Sale Credit Qualification

Normal qualification:

> 100% verified Payment Received.

Alternative:

> Approved Buyback after source Booking had already reached at least 25% Payment Received.

If Buyback route qualifies the credit:

- credit may become `QUALIFIED`;
- actual Trip booking/fulfilment remains blocked until Stable Buyback Completion.

---

# 28. Reference Credit — New Logic

No monetary Invite exists.

Reference rules:

- immediate Membership inviter only;
- no multi-level;
- no joining credit;
- each directly introduced Member can contribute one lifetime Reference Credit;
- self-purchase does not consume Reference opportunity;
- only first Reference-eligible third-party sale inside an active Trip Programme can consume it;
- sale in a Project with Trip Disabled does not consume it;
- multi-unit first qualifying transaction gives seller multiple Own-Sale Credits but inviter only one Reference Credit.

---

# 29. Reference Candidate and Atomic Consumption

Reference opportunity must be concurrency safe.

At qualification milestone:

1. verify introduced Member's lifetime Reference opportunity is still unused;
2. verify sale is third-party, Reference-eligible and Trip-enabled under frozen version;
3. acquire row lock / use atomic conditional update;
4. apply tie-breaker if competing qualification timestamps exist;
5. create exactly one Reference Credit;
6. mark opportunity consumed;
7. persist winning Booking/Project.

Tie-breaker:

1. earliest verified qualification timestamp;
2. if exact equal, lower permanent Booking Number.

Payment timing may influence which Project wins. Do not replace this with Booking Request order.

---

# 30. Reference Credit for Deactivated Inviter

If inviter is Deactivated when the introduced Member's qualifying event occurs:

- consume the lifetime Reference opportunity;
- create the Reference Credit;
- place credit/fulfilment on hold;
- do not reopen opportunity;
- release usable status only after valid Member reactivation and other holds clear.

# 32. Trip Bucket Opening / Freeze

A bucket opens when:

> the Member receives the first valid Pending Trip Credit from an Accounts-approved Booking Request in that Project.

Freeze into the bucket:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- self-purchase rule snapshot;
- eligible inventory/programme identity;
- Trip Programme Version;
- Trip Terms Version.

If the credit that opened the bucket later reverses and no other valid Pending/Qualified credit remains, close that empty bucket. The next valid credit opens a new bucket under the then-current applicable Programme Version.

This prevents a throwaway/rejected transaction from permanently locking easier rules.

---

# 33. Trip Bucket Completion

A bucket is Earned when:

- total allocated Qualified credits >= Total Target;
- Own-Sale allocated count >= Minimum Own;
- Reference allocated count <= Maximum Reference;
- credits are not Expired/Reversed;
- credit fulfilment holds do not prevent reward fulfilment.

Reference is optional; Own-Sale may satisfy the whole target.

Use deterministic FIFO allocation.

---

# 34. FIFO Allocation

Allocation order:

- earliest Qualified eligible credits first;
- respect credit type constraints;
- respect Minimum Own / Maximum Reference;
- used credits cannot be reused.

Recommended approach:

- allocate Own credits needed to satisfy Minimum Own;
- fill remaining target using earliest eligible Own/Reference credits while never exceeding Max Reference;
- preserve deterministic ordering by `qualifiedAt`, then permanent ID.

Do not give Members manual cherry-picking rights unless future rule changes.

---

# 35. Banked Credits and Expiry

Qualified but unused Trip Credits:

- remain banked within the same Project/programme;
- expire 12 months after qualification;
- cannot move to another Project;
- cannot be reused after allocation to a fulfilled reward.

Scheduled expiry must be idempotent.

Already Earned Trip Rewards use Trip Terms redemption deadlines, not the generic 12-month credit-expiry rule.

---

# 36. Trip Programme Closure

When programme closes:

- no post-cut-off approved Booking can create a new eligible credit under that programme;
- existing frozen eligible activity may continue according to disclosed closure rules;
- open bucket gets a final wind-down/completion deadline from Trip Terms;
- no silent retroactive deletion of valid progress.

Store closure/cut-off metadata in programme/version configuration or linked Terms reference.

# 39. Trip Reward Reversal / Deficiency

## Before Earned

Invalid credit reverses and progress recalculates.

## Earned but not Booked

If a used credit becomes invalid:

- attempt FIFO backfill from unused valid Qualified credits;
- if not enough, mark Reward/Bucket `DEFICIENT`;
- hold fulfilment until target becomes valid.

## Booked

Trip Terms control cancellation/rebooking.

## Travelled

- no ordinary reward clawback;
- underlying credits remain consumed forever;
- fraud may create Recovery/disciplinary action but no reopening of reward opportunities.

# 40. Royalty Relationship — New Source Rule

Remove Enquiry-source ownership from Royalty entitlement.

Royalty relationship is determined from the Primary Customer's earliest qualifying first purchase.

At approved first Booking:

- Sold By Member → create/maintain Provisional Royalty Linked Member = selling Member;
- Sold By Customer / 3% Club → no Member Royalty relationship.

Only Primary Customer creates the relationship.

Additional Customers do not each create Royalty relationships.

# 42. Royalty Relationship Finalisation

Finalise when first qualifying purchase reaches:

- 100% Payment Received; or
- Approved Buyback after source Booking had already reached at least 25% Payment Received.

If first Booking cancels before either qualifying milestone:

- provisional relationship becomes Cancelled/historical;
- opportunity remains available for a later genuine first qualifying purchase.

Do not delete history.

# 43. Royalty Reward Trigger

A Final Royalty Linked Member may receive one Royalty Credit when that Primary Customer later makes the first qualifying direct personal purchase through 3% Club.

Required:

- relationship Final;
- opportunity UNUSED;
- reward-triggering Booking is a later personal purchase;
- final Sold By = 3% Club;
- no selling Member closes that sale;
- Royalty Gift Programme is live/approved;
- other eligibility/holds clear.

If final Sold By = Member:

- no Royalty event on that Booking;
- opportunity remains UNUSED.

---

# 47. Royalty Buyback Qualification / Fulfilment

Approved Buyback may make Royalty Credit eligible only if source reward-triggering Booking had already reached >=25% Payment Received.

If qualification route = Buyback:

Gift fulfilment remains blocked until either:

- underlying Customer purchase independently reaches 100% Payment Received; or
- Buyback reaches Stable Buyback Completion.

If Buyback unwinds before fulfilment and normal milestone has not independently occurred:

- reverse Buyback-based eligibility;
- do not create duplicate/replacement reward automatically.

---

# 48. Royalty Gift Finality

After Gift Delivered:

- opportunity remains consumed forever;

# 94. Accepted Risks — Developer Guardrails

The following must **not** be “fixed” without a future approved Change Request:

1. Related-party Member sale may still be normal third-party sale.
2. Sold By Customer may be related to buyer or Additional Customer if otherwise eligible.
3. Primary Customer Change does not automatically re-run old benefit economics.
4. Second personal Booking may qualify as repeat purchase immediately.
5. Reciprocal Customer closing is allowed.
6. Full Direct is eligible at 25% Payment Received.
7. Only formal Authorised Discount reduces commission base.
8. General buyer KYC need not block Booking approval.
9. Final Sold By selection alone controls Club-direct repeat treatment.
10. Original sale participant may later earn Buying Commission if normal rules are met.
11. Reference Project may be influenced by qualification timing.

Fraud/sham/collusion controls still apply.
