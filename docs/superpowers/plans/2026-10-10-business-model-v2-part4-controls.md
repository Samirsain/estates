## Readings of the docs (no new rules)

1. **Bank uniqueness (CP §57; SSOT §94).** Every bank detail carries a keyed blind index of its normalised identity — the bank (IFSC's four letters) and the account digits, so another branch of the same bank is the same account. An account already verified for another, unmerged Person cannot be saved unless it carries proof of the joint holders; it then waits as Pending for a separate decision by a different Accounts or MD account (CP §78 "separately approved"; UAT CTL-09 "Accounts/MD approve exception"). Two entries of the same account are serialised by a lock on the index (SYS-11).
2. **Staff conflict (CP §58; SSOT §95).** A conflicted beneficiary is a Person with a staff account, or a declared spouse, parent, child or sibling of one (Terms 6.1 "immediate family"). Every Direct, Loyalty, Buying Commission, Trip Reward and Royalty Credit of a conflicted Person is held ("Staff conflict review") until MD approves it (NT09). A conflicted staff user cannot decide, pay or fulfil their own or their relative's benefit; the refusal is audited (CTL-11). A declaration ended before MD decides cancels the review. *Owner, 10 Oct: the Staff relatives section is removed from Administration;* the declaration model and service stay, and a staff Person's own benefits are still held.
3. **Circumvention (CP §55; SSOT §92).** Before a benefit is released, the CRM looks for another Person with a Recovery Outstanding who shares a bank account, the primary mobile, or the address. On a match it opens one review per Recovery (NT08, Accounts) and holds the benefit; nobody is denied automatically. Accounts or MD clears it (released) or restricts it (held while that Recovery is outstanding), with a reason. A "known related-party link" has no record of its own in the CRM, so only the three data indicators are checked.
4. **Third-party payer (SSOT §22; CP §15).** Payment Received may record who paid when it was not the buyer, and their reference. Optional; kept for review; carried onto a correction.
5. **Merge (CP §87; SSOT §100).** The Customer-closing count and Recovery already read merged identities; the closing count is stored rebuilt and capped at 3. Of two consumed Royalty or Reference opportunities, the later unfulfilled one is reversed and the survivor carries the earlier; a merged-away identity earns no new Royalty. An Own-Sale Credit for the same unit and programme is kept once. Fulfilled rewards are never touched.
6. **Screens (CP §8, §67, §68–§72, §90).** Rewards page gains a Reviews tab (NT08/NT09 decisions); a dashboard card counts open reviews; bank forms take the joint-account proof; the payment dialog takes the payer. The Commercial & Rewards settings card and form both follow CP §8's sections — Direct, Loyalty, Trip, Reward Programme References, Economics Review, Version — with the field names of CP §7.1, approver and date shown, and full read-only history.
7. **Integrity (UAT §28).** `npm run reconcile` adds `business_model_v2_integrity` for the invariants the database does not already refuse.
8. **Mock data (UAT §6; CP §88).** `npm run seed:mock-v2` builds the UAT dataset through the services, additively and re-runnably, and writes its key to `docss/9. Business_Model_v2_Mock_Data_Key.md`.
