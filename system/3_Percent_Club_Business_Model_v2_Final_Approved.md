# 3% CLUB REAL ESTATE — BUSINESS MODEL v2
## Final Approved Business Model

**Document version:** 2.0  
**Document date:** 8 October 2026  
**Business Effective Date:** Production go-live date/time in Asia/Kolkata  
**Status:** FINAL BUSINESS-RULE BASELINE  
**Brand:** 3% Club Real Estate / 3% Real Estate Club

> 8 October 2026 is the document date, not the commercial Effective Date. Business Model v2 becomes effective only at production go-live after Terms/PPT, CRM changes, UAT and staff readiness are complete.

---

# 1. Purpose

This document is the new business-side single source of truth for 3% Club.

The new live model removes:

- monetary Member Invite percentage bands;
- Invite positions and anniversary/performance cycles;
- monetary Royalty percentage bands;
- Royalty positions and anniversary/performance cycles;
- the lifetime limit of three Customer Loyalty benefits;
- the requirement that a Customer must become a Member after three Loyalty benefits;
- the assumption that every Project pays exactly 3% Direct;
- the combined 4% sale-commission cap.

Only mock/test data exists under the earlier model, so no live legacy-transition engine is required.

# 10. One final Sold By classification

Each sale has one final approved classification:

1. Sold By Member
2. Sold By Customer
3. Sold By 3% Club

Only one final sale classification applies.

---

# 11. Hard combination rule

The old 4% monetary cap is removed.

However:

> **A single sale action cannot generate both Project Direct Commission and Customer Loyalty to different closers.**

| Sale classification | Monetary benefit |
|---|---|
| Sold By Member | Project Direct |
| Member self-purchase | Buyer-Member Project Direct |
| Sold By Customer | Project Customer Loyalty |
| Club-direct qualifying repeat personal purchase | Project Customer Loyalty |

Trip and Royalty Gift are non-cash rewards and may coexist only under their own rules.

Buying Commission is separate.

# 12. Project settings

Each Project may independently configure:

## Monetary
- Direct Commission — Enabled / Disabled
- Direct rate
- Customer Loyalty — Enabled / Disabled
- Loyalty rate

## Trip
- Trip Programme — Enabled / Disabled
- Total Trip Target
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits
- Maximum Reference Credits
- eligible/excluded inventory
- Trip Programme Version
- Trip Terms/PPT Version

Direct, Loyalty and Trip are independent settings.

---

# 13. Rate ceilings

Hard maximums:

> **Direct Commission: 5% maximum**

> **Customer Loyalty: 3% maximum**

Normal rule:

> **Customer Loyalty should be lower than Direct Commission.**

MD may approve an exception with written reason and audit.

A benefit that is not offered should be explicitly **Disabled**, not silently entered as 0%.

---

# 14. Project settings approval

1. Admin prepares.
2. MD reviews.
3. MD approves.
4. Approved version becomes Active.

No silent overwrite is allowed.

Every material change creates a new immutable version.

---

# 15. Project settings versioning

A Project version preserves at minimum:

- Direct Enabled/Disabled and rate;
- Loyalty Enabled/Disabled and rate;
- Trip Enabled/Disabled;
- Total Target;
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits;
- Maximum Reference Credits;
- eligible inventory;
- Trip Programme/Terms Version;
- effective date/time;
- approver;
- reason.

Old versions remain historical.

---

# 16. Booking freeze point

Applicable financial/reward rules freeze at:

> **Booking Request submission**

Freeze:

- Direct setting/rate;
- Loyalty setting/rate;
- Project Commission Settings Version;
- Trip Programme eligibility/version;
- eligible inventory treatment;
- applicable programme terms.

Later Project changes do not change that Booking.

---

# 17. No retroactive eligibility

A Booking/Unit gets only the reward rules active and applicable when its Booking Request was submitted.

If Trip was disabled when the Booking Request was submitted and enabled later, that Booking does not retroactively earn Trip Credit.

The same principle applies to later rate increases or programme improvements.

---

# 18. Commissionable sale value

Keep the global formula:

> **Commissionable Sale Value = (Final Approved Base Property Value + Applicable PLC) − Authorised Discount**

Exclude GST/taxes, stamp duty, registration, government levies, documentation, maintenance, utilities, finance charges, interest, penalties and other pass-through charges.

Only the rate becomes Project-specific.

---

# 19. Project Direct — third-party Member sale

When:

- Member is Active;
- final Sold By = Member;
- Project Direct is Enabled;
- transaction is otherwise eligible;

the selling Member earns the frozen Project Direct rate.

Normal milestone:

> **25% verified Payment Received**

Direct may be earned on unlimited qualifying third-party sales.

Only one selling Member receives Direct on one sale.

---

# 20. Member self-purchase

Self-purchase applies only when:

> **Primary Customer is an Active Member.**

An Additional Customer who is a Member does not by itself make the Booking a Member self-purchase.

Rules:

- buyer-Member receives the frozen Project Direct rate if Direct is Enabled;
- Direct milestone = **100% Payment Received**;
- no second selling Member receives Direct;
- no Customer Loyalty applies;
- self-purchase may create Own-Sale Trip Credits;
- self-purchase does not create Reference Credit for the Member's inviter;
- Buyback does not accelerate self-purchase Direct.

---

# 21. Customer Loyalty

Each Project has one shared Loyalty rate for both eligible routes:

1. Customer closes a qualifying sale for another buyer.
2. Customer makes an eligible repeat direct personal purchase.

Customer Loyalty is:

> **unlimited in lifetime quantity**

But:

> **One Booking can generate at most one Customer Loyalty entitlement for the eligible Customer.**

Normal milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Approved Buyback remains an approved alternative milestone under the Buyback rules.

---

# 22. Customer-closing Loyalty

Applies when:

- final Sold By = Customer;
- Customer genuinely closes the sale;
- Project Loyalty is Enabled;
- other eligibility rules are satisfied.

The Project's frozen Loyalty rate applies.

---

# 23. Repeat-purchase Loyalty

A Customer's eligible repeat direct personal purchase may earn the frozen Project Loyalty rate.

Rules:

- first personal purchase is not repeat-purchase Loyalty;
- Member-closed repeat purchase gets no Customer Loyalty;
- Project Loyalty must be Enabled;
- normal milestone 100% Payment Received;
- Approved Buyback may qualify it under the Buyback rule.

---

# 24. Member status and Customer Loyalty

An Active Member cannot use Sold By Customer.

A Person who has Member capability but is currently Deactivated cannot earn either Loyalty route:

- Customer-closing Loyalty;
- repeat-personal-purchase Loyalty.

Existing properly eligible protected Booking Requests keep their frozen treatment.

Deactivation cannot be used to switch reward systems.

---

# 25. Membership invitation after repeated Customer sales

After the Customer's third successful **Customer-closing Loyalty** event:

> trigger a non-blocking Membership invitation/review.

This does not stop future Loyalty.

Membership remains optional.

---

# 26. Customer → Member conversion

A Customer may apply to become a Member at any time.

If a valid inviter exists:

> inviter freezes at **Membership Application submission**.

Later correction requires Admin/MD, reason and audit.

If none exists, no inviter is assigned.

The Member who sold property to the Customer or owns the Royalty relationship is not automatically the Membership inviter.

Past Customer activity remains historical and does not create retrospective Member benefits.

---

# 27. Sales & Reference Trip Reward

The Trip Programme rewards:

1. genuine Project sales;
2. genuine first qualifying sale performance by directly introduced Members.

It is not a joining or multi-level reward.

Joining alone gives:

> **zero Trip progress**

---

# 28. Trip target composition

Each Trip-enabled Project defines:

- Total Target
- Minimum Own-Sale Credits
- Maximum Reference Credits

Example:

> Target 9 / Minimum Own 6 / Maximum Reference 3

Eligible combinations include:

- 9 Own + 0 Reference
- 8 + 1
- 7 + 2
- 6 + 3

Not eligible:

- 5 Own + 4 Reference

Reference Credits are optional.

---

# 29. Own-Sale Credit is Unit/Plot-based

> **Each distinct qualifying Plot/Unit = one Own-Sale Credit.**

Five qualifying units sold together may create five Own-Sale Credits.

Trip progress is based on qualifying units, not merely Booking Number structure.

---

# 30. Normal Trip qualification

Normal qualification:

> **100% verified Payment Received**

Before qualification:

> Pending Trip Credit

At qualification:

> Qualified Trip Credit

---

# 31. Member self-purchase Trip Credits

Qualifying self-purchased units count toward Own-Sale progress.

There is no general limit on how many self-purchase credits may satisfy the Own-Sale requirement.

However:

> **The same Member can receive only one self-purchase Trip Credit for the same Plot/Unit within the same Project Trip Programme.**

This prevents repeated self-purchase → Buyback → self-purchase reward farming.

A later genuine third-party sale of the Buyback unit may still create a new Own-Sale Credit for the genuine seller.

---

# 32. Reference Credit

Rules:

- immediate inviter only;
- no multi-level credit;
- one directly introduced Member can contribute **one lifetime Reference Credit**;
- self-purchase by introduced Member does not consume the Reference opportunity;
- the opportunity is global across Projects;
- it is consumed only by the first Reference-eligible sale in an **active Trip Programme**.

A sale in a Project with no Trip Programme does not consume it.

---

# 33. First referred sale with multiple units

If introduced Member B's first Reference-eligible transaction includes five qualifying units:

- B may earn up to five Own-Sale Credits;
- B's immediate inviter receives **one Reference Credit only**.

---

# 34. Reference qualification

Reference Credit qualifies when the introduced Member's first Reference-eligible third-party sale reaches:

- 100% verified Payment Received; or
- Approved Buyback alternative milestone.

Rejected/non-qualifying activity does not consume the opportunity.

Tie-breaker:

1. earliest verified qualifying timestamp;
2. if equal, lower permanent Booking Number.

---

# 35. One sale may create two Members' credits

A first qualifying referred sale can separately create:

- Own-Sale Credit(s) for the seller;
- one Reference Credit for the immediate inviter.

These are separate entitlements.

---

# 36. Extra Reference Credits

If a current bucket allows maximum 3 Reference Credits and Member has more:

> extra qualified Reference Credits remain banked for that same Project.

They may be used in later Trip buckets subject to the later bucket rules.

They are not lost.

---

# 37. FIFO allocation

Credits are allocated automatically:

> **earliest qualified eligible credits first**

subject to:

- credit type;
- Minimum Own requirement;
- Maximum Reference allowance;
- frozen Project programme rules.

Used credits cannot be reused.

---

# 38. Repeatable Trips

Once a bucket is completed:

- Trip Reward becomes Earned;
- used credits are locked;
- fresh/unused credits may build the next bucket.

Each fresh completed target can earn another Trip while the applicable Project programme remains active under its terms.

---

# 39. Trip bucket freeze

A Member's current Project Trip bucket opens and freezes when the Member receives the:

> **first eligible Pending Trip Credit**

normally at qualifying Booking Request submission.

Freeze:

- Total Target;
- Minimum Own;
- Maximum Reference;
- self-purchase rules;
- eligible inventory;
- Trip Programme Version;
- Trip Terms/PPT Version.

Future Project changes do not alter that active bucket.

Next bucket uses the then-current programme version.

---

# 40. Trip programme closure

When a Trip Programme closes:

- no post-cut-off Booking Request becomes eligible;
- protected pre-cut-off Pending Credits remain under their frozen version;
- protected qualifying activity may still complete the frozen bucket according to disclosed closure terms.

Partial progress is not silently cancelled retroactively.

---

# 41. Approved Buyback as Trip qualification

Approved Buyback can qualify the **source sale's Trip Credit** before source Payment Received reaches 100%.

The credit belongs to the source sale:

- source Sold By Member → source selling Member gets Own-Sale Credit(s);
- eligible first Reference sale → immediate inviter may receive one Reference Credit;
- Member self-purchase may receive its Own-Sale Credit subject to the one-credit-per-Member/unit/programme anti-recycling rule;
- Buying Commission beneficiary gets no Trip Credit merely for arranging Buyback;
- ordinary cancellation does not create Trip Credit.

Buyback does not accelerate Direct Commission.

---

# 42. Buyback-qualified credit: Qualified vs usable

Approved Buyback may make the Trip Credit:

> **Qualified**

But a Buyback-qualified credit becomes usable for actual Trip booking only after:

> **Stable Buyback Completion**

---

# 43. Stable Buyback Completion

Stable Buyback Completion means all applicable conditions are true:

1. Buyback/Acquisition is formally Approved.
2. Payment Given = 100% verified.
3. Buyback/Acquisition has not been cancelled or unwound.
4. Applicable completion/document-return work is complete, including where relevant:
   - Allotment papers collected back;
   - Registry-back completed;
   - other required acquisition completion conditions.

At Stable Buyback Completion:

> Buyback-qualified Trip Credits become usable for Trip fulfilment.

---

# 44. Buyback unwind and Trip Credit

If Buyback later unwinds before Stable Buyback Completion:

- Buyback-created Trip qualification reverses;
- source-sale status is recalculated;
- if the source independently reaches normal 100%, normal qualification may still apply;
- no duplicate credit is created.

---

# 45. Trip cancellation/reversal

## Before Reward Earned
Invalid credit reverses and progress recalculates.

## Earned but not Booked
If a used credit becomes invalid:
- reward can become Deficient;
- next unused eligible qualified credit may backfill under FIFO;
- otherwise fulfilment pauses.

## Trip Booked
Published Trip Terms control cancellation/rebooking/traveller changes.

## Travel Completed
No ordinary business-event clawback.

Fraud/sham activity remains separately actionable.

---

# 46. Trip reward owner and nominee

The earning Member remains the owner of:

- Own-Sale Credits;
- Reference Credits;
- Trip Reward entitlement.

Credits do not transfer ownership.

Subject to published Trip Terms:

> the earning Member may nominate another traveller before fulfilment.

No automatic cash alternative exists.

---

# 47. Recovery / deactivation and Trip fulfilment

Recovery Outstanding:
- progress remains;
- earned reward remains;
- fulfilment is held until Recovery Cleared.

Deactivated Member:
- reward/history remains;
- fulfilment is held until valid reactivation.

---

# 48. Royalty Relationship Reward

Royalty is now:

> **a one-time non-cash relationship reward**

not a monetary percentage.

No:

- Royalty percentage;
- band;
- position;
- annual reset;
- performance cycle.

---

# 49. Royalty relationship creation

At the Customer's earliest approved first Booking:

### Sold By Member
Selling Member becomes the **Provisional Royalty Linked Member**.

### Sold By Customer / Sold By 3% Club
No Member Royalty relationship is created.

The relationship becomes final when that first purchase reaches:

- 100% verified Payment Received; or
- Approved Buyback.

---

# 50. Cancellation before Royalty relationship finalises

If the first Booking is cancelled before both:

- 100% Payment Received; and
- Approved Buyback;

then:

- provisional Royalty relationship is removed from current entitlement;
- opportunity is not consumed;
- a later genuine first qualifying purchase may establish the relationship.

History remains.

---

# 51. First purchase not Member-sold

If the Customer's first qualifying purchase was Sold By Customer or Sold By 3% Club:

> a later Member sale does not retroactively create a Royalty relationship.

---

# 52. Royalty reward event

A final Royalty Linked Member may receive one Royalty Relationship Reward when the Customer later makes the first qualifying:

> **direct personal purchase through 3% Club**

provided the Royalty opportunity is unused and no selling Member closes that repeat purchase.

If another Member closes it:

- no Royalty Reward on that sale;
- unused Royalty opportunity remains.

---

# 53. One Royalty opportunity per Customer

Each Customer may create:

> **one Royalty Credit for life**

for the valid Royalty Linked Member.

---

# 54. One Royalty Credit = one Gift entitlement

One qualifying Royalty event creates:

> one Royalty Credit

which creates:

> one catalogue Gift entitlement.

Credits do not combine into a points system unless a later approved programme expressly adds that feature.

---

# 55. Royalty Gift Programme Version

The applicable Gift Programme Version freezes when the:

> **qualifying future Royalty Booking Request is submitted**

not when the original Royalty relationship was created.

Later catalogue changes do not alter the frozen entitlement.

---

# 56. Royalty catalogue

The full Gift catalogue stays outside CRM in the official PPT/Programme Terms.

Before live launch define:

- exact gift choices;
- specifications;
- phone model if applicable;
- gold weight/purity/certification if applicable;
- substitution;
- stock unavailability;
- redemption deadline;
- delivery;
- warranty/support;
- tax/statutory wording;
- nomination;
- cancellation.

No live Royalty Gift promise may be activated without an approved dated Programme Version.

---

# 57. Royalty data in CRM

CRM records/reference:

- Royalty relationship;
- Royalty Credit ID;
- Reward Programme Version;
- selected Reward reference;
- selection date;
- Eligible / Ordered / Delivered / Cancelled-Reversed status;
- fulfilment reference;
- delivery date.

CRM does not need the full catalogue or reward cost.

---

# 58. Royalty + Buyback

Buyback itself does not earn Royalty.

Approved Buyback is only an alternative milestone on the underlying qualifying Customer purchase.

Royalty Credit belongs to:

> the already-established Royalty Linked Member

not the person arranging the Buyback or the Buying Commission beneficiary.

---

# 59. Buyback-triggered Royalty fulfilment

Approved Buyback may make the Royalty Credit **Eligible** early.

Physical Gift fulfilment waits until either:

1. underlying qualifying Customer purchase independently reaches 100% Payment Received; or
2. Buyback reaches Stable Buyback Completion.

If Buyback unwinds before fulfilment and no normal milestone exists, the Buyback-created eligibility reverses.

---

# 60. Royalty Gift after delivery

After delivery:

- no ordinary post-delivery clawback unless published Terms specifically define one;
- fraud, sham activity or material misrepresentation remains separately recoverable/disciplinary.

Before delivery, invalidated eligibility may reverse the entitlement.

---

# 61. Royalty reward owner and nominee

The Royalty Linked Member remains owner of the Royalty Credit/reward.

Underlying credit ownership does not transfer.

Subject to published terms:

> Member may nominate another gift recipient before fulfilment.

No automatic cash alternative exists.

---

# 62. Customer Loyalty + Royalty Reward

A qualifying Club-direct repeat personal purchase may create:

1. Customer Loyalty to the buyer; and
2. one non-cash Royalty Reward to the Royalty Linked Member.

These are separate benefits.

---

# 63. Buying Commission

Buying Commission remains separate.

Rate:

> percentage of Acquisition Price

Hard maximum:

> **5%**

One beneficiary may be:

- Member;
- Customer;
- external broker.

Restrictions remain against the seller/returning owner earning Buying Commission merely for arranging their own return.

---

# 64. Buying Commission freeze point

Buying Commission freezes at:

> **formal Acquisition approval**

Any later rate change requires controlled correction, reason, approval and before/after history.

---

# 65. Buying Commission milestone

Normal milestone:

> **100% verified Payment Given**

Paid Early:

> MD approval only

No Trip Credit is earned merely because someone arranged the Buyback.

---

# 66. Payment Received vs Payment Given

## Payment Received
Customer-side sale progress.

Used for:
- Direct;
- Loyalty;
- normal Trip qualification;
- normal Royalty reward qualification.

## Payment Given
Company acquisition progress.

Used for:
- acquisition;
- Buying Commission;
- Stable Buyback Completion;
- Buyback-qualified reward fulfilment.

Never mix them.

---

# 67. Recovery

If a paid monetary benefit later becomes invalid:

- create Recovery Outstanding;
- keep external Accounts Recovery Reference;
- exact rupee recovery can remain outside CRM.

Recovery deadline:

> **15 calendar days from notice**

Unresolved recovery may lead to Membership deactivation under the approved policy.

Recovery Outstanding blocks Trip/Gift fulfilment.

# 72. Fraud / artificial activity

Fraudulent, sham, circular or artificially structured transactions do not qualify.

Examples:

- fake Members;
- duplicate identities;
- test Bookings;
- fabricated Customers;
- false Sold By;
- circular reward farming;
- repeated self-purchase/Buyback on the same unit by the same Member.

# 74. Same-Project Change Plot

Same Booking continues.

Therefore:

- frozen Direct/Loyalty version remains;
- Trip programme treatment remains;
- credit follows the final valid unit outcome without duplication.

# 75. Sold By correction

Sold By correction may change:

- Direct;
- Loyalty;
- Own-Sale Credit;
- Reference Credit;
- Royalty relationship;
- Royalty Reward.

Recalculate using the Booking's frozen versions, not today's Project settings.

# 76. Payment correction

Payment corrections must not create duplicate rewards.

If later restored, the same entitlement re-qualifies without duplication.

# 77. Identity merge

# 83. Final approved rule summary

## Project Direct
- Project-specific
- Max 5%
- May be Disabled
- Frozen at Booking Request submission
- Third-party sale: 25% Payment Received milestone
- Member self-purchase: 100%
- Buyback does not accelerate Direct

## Customer Loyalty
- Project-specific
- Max 3%
- Normally below Direct; MD exception allowed
- May be Disabled
- Same rate for both Loyalty routes
- Unlimited lifetime quantity
- One Loyalty entitlement per Booking
- First personal purchase excluded
- Member-closed repeat purchase excluded
- Deactivated Member cannot use either Loyalty route
- Third Customer-closing event triggers non-blocking Membership invitation
- Approved Buyback alternative milestone retained

## Trip
- Project-specific and independently enabled
- Own-Sale = qualifying Plot/Unit
- One lifetime Reference Credit per directly introduced Member
- First Reference opportunity consumed only in active Trip Programme
- Seller + inviter can both receive separate qualifying credits
- Project sets Total / Minimum Own / Maximum Reference
- Extra Reference Credits banked in same Project
- Self-purchase counts
- Same Member cannot repeatedly earn self-purchase credit from the same unit/programme
- Normal milestone 100% Payment Received
- Approved Buyback may alternatively qualify source-sale Trip Credit
- Buyback credit usable only after Stable Buyback Completion
- FIFO
- No credit reuse
- Bucket freezes on first Pending eligible credit
- Repeatable
- Recovery/Deactivation hold fulfilment
- nominee allowed; credit ownership does not transfer
- no automatic cash alternative

## Royalty Relationship Reward
- Non-cash
- No percentage/bands/cycles
- First-purchase relationship logic retained
- One opportunity per Customer
- Future qualifying Club-direct personal purchase only
- Another Member's repeat sale does not consume it
- One Royalty Credit = one Gift entitlement
- Gift Programme Version freezes at qualifying future Booking Request
- Approved Buyback may create early eligibility
- fulfilment waits for source 100% or Stable Buyback Completion
- nominee allowed; credit ownership does not transfer
- no automatic cash alternative

## Buying Commission
- Percentage of Acquisition Price
- Max 5%
- One eligible beneficiary
- Frozen at Acquisition approval
- 100% Payment Given milestone
- Paid Early only MD-approved

## Global
- No combined 4% monetary cap
- Direct and Loyalty cannot both apply to the same sale
- global commissionable base formula retained
- Project settings versioned and MD-approved
- no retroactive eligibility
- Project economics reviewed outside CRM
- Effective Date = production go-live
- no legacy transition engine required

# 84. Final business principle

> **Every monetary rate, sales credit, Reference credit and non-cash reward has one owner, one qualification event, one frozen programme version, one cancellation rule and one fulfilment rule. Nothing is changed retroactively after the Member has acted under a disclosed rule.**

Core brand message:

> **YOUR BUSINESS RELATIONSHIPS SHOULD GROW BEYOND ONE DEAL.**
