## 1. Ek line mein business

> **3% Club plots bechta hai — aur property dealers ko free mein Member banaakar unke through bechta hai. Dealer ko har sale par 3% likhit commission milta hai, aur poora hisaab ek CRM system mein transparent rehta hai.**

Company ki apni sales team chhoti rehti hai. Uski asli sales force **shehar-shehar ke property dealers** hain, jo Member bankar Club ke plots bechte hain.

---

## 2. Problem jo hum solve karte hain (Indian market)

| Dealer ki problem | Customer ki problem | Company ki problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log "broker" samajhte hain, brand nahi banta | Dealer pe bharosa nahi — fraud ka darr | Har shehar mein sales team rakhna mehenga |
| Builder commission bolta kuch hai, deta kuch aur, late deta hai | Paisa kiske account mein jaa raha, pata nahi | Dealers ka hisaab Excel/diary mein, jhagde hote hain |
| Bechne ke liye apna saaf stock nahi | Official receipt nahi milti | Ek plot do dealers bech dete hain (double commitment) |
| Laaya hua customer agli baar seedha builder ke paas | Kaagaz aur registry ki tension | Commission double payment ka risk |

**3% Club ka jawab:** ek system jismein dealer ko **pehchaan + saaf inventory + likhit commission** milta hai, customer ko **official process** milta hai, aur company ko **bina salary ke sales network** milta hai.

## 3. Kaun-kaun hai is business mein

### 3.1 Company (3% Club)
- Plotted projects chalata hai — **kai Projects aur kai Developers** ke saath.
- Plot types: **Residential, Commercial, Informal Sector**.
- Sirf **individual buyers** ko bechta hai (company/organisation buyer nahi).
- Purani property **wapas khareedta** hai (Buyback) aur bahar se property **khareedkar dobara bechta** hai (Purchase for Resale).
- Zameen ki leads bhi leta hai (**Land Inquiry**) — naye projects ke liye.

### 3.2 Member (Property Dealer / Associate)
- **Membership bilkul free** — koi joining fee nahi, koi monthly charge nahi.
- Independent business associate hai — employee, partner ya franchisee **nahi**.
- Customer laata hai, deal close karta hai, commission kamaata hai.
- Member ID, Member portal, approved inventory aur project material milta hai.

### 3.3 Customer (Kharidaar)
- Plot khareedta hai.
- Agar woh kisi aur ko plot dilwaaye, ya khud dobara khareede, to use bhi **Loyalty commission** milta hai.
- Customer baad mein chahe to Member bhi ban sakta hai.

### 3.4 Company ki andar ki team (CRM ke roles)

| Role | Kaam |
| --- | --- |
| **MD** | Sabse bada authority — exception approvals, Paid Early, merge |
| **Admin** | Projects, plots, users, Member activate/deactivate |
| **Accounts** | Booking approve/reject, payment confirm, commission payout, bank verify |
| **CRM** | Enquiry, Hold, Booking request, dealer/customer follow-up, calling |
| **MIS** | Reports (sirf dekh sakta hai) |
| **PC** | Project aur plot ki taiyaari |

**Maker-checker rule:** jo request banaata hai, woh khud approve nahi kar sakta. Booking CRM banaata hai, Accounts approve karta hai.

## 4. Paisa kahan se aata hai (Revenue)

| Source | Kaise |
| --- | --- |
| **Plot sale** | Company apne projects ke plots bechti hai — yahi main kamaai hai |
| **Resale (Buyback)** | Apna becha hua plot customer se wapas khareedkar dobara bechna |
| **Purchase for Resale** | Bahar se property khareedkar Club ki inventory mein daalna aur bechna |
| **Land deals** | Land Inquiry se zameen lekar naye projects banana |

**Kharcha (commission):** ek sale par saari sale commissions milaakar **maximum 4%**. Isse zyada system allow hi nahi karta.

> Company ka margin (plot ki kharid-bikri ka farak) CRM mein record nahi hota — CRM sirf percentage rakhta hai. Margin ka hisaab Accounts bahar rakhta hai.

### Ek sale ka hisaab (₹50 lakh ka plot, example)

| Kise | Kitna | Rupaye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Becha jis Member ne (Direct) | 3% | ₹1,50,000 |
| Us Member ko jisne use Club mein joda (Invite, position 1–3) | 1% | ₹50,000 |
| **Company ka total commission kharcha** | **4%** | **₹2,00,000** |
| Company ke paas bacha | 96% | ₹48,00,000 |

Commission kis rakam par lagta hai:

> **Commissionable Sale Value = (Base Property Value + PLC) − Authorised Discount**

GST, stamp duty, registration, documentation, maintenance, interest, penalty — in par commission **nahi** lagta.

## 5. Dealer ko Club tak laane ka tareeka (Growth Funnel)

```
WhatsApp message (Funnel 1A / 1B / 1C)
        │  "Aapka naam brand hai ya sirf dealer?"
        ▼
Dealer "BRAND" button dabata hai
        │  (nahi dabaya → Funnel 2A / 2B / 2C follow-up → Funnel 3 last message)
        ▼
CRM team call karti hai
        ▼
Dealer details deta hai (Aadhaar, PAN, bank, RERA)
        ▼
Admin/MD review karke Member activate karta hai  →  Member ID + portal login
        ▼
Member customer laata hai  →  Enquiry → Hold → Booking
        ▼
Payment aata hai  →  commission eligible  →  7 working days mein payout
        ▼
Khush Member apne dealer dost laata hai (Invite)  →  network badhta hai
```

**Funnel ka core message:** paise ki baat nahi, **pehchaan** ki baat — *"Shehar mein property ki baat aaye to naam aapka yaad aaye."* Commission call par samjhaaya jaata hai.

## 6. Plot bikne ka poora safar

| Step | Kya hota hai | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Enquiry** | Customer ki interest record hoti hai | Source likha jaata hai (Member, Customer, walk-in, online, site visit). Enquiry se commission **tay nahi hota** |
| **2. Hold** | Plot customer ke liye rok diya jaata hai | **72 ghante**. Ek customer ke max **3 open plot** ek saath. Member Hold request daalta hai, CRM approve karta hai |
| **3. Booking Request** | CRM booking form bharta hai | Primary Customer, plot, PLC %, **Sold By** (kisne becha), payment schedule |
| **4. Accounts Approval** | Accounts approve ya reject karta hai | Approve hote hi commission lines ban jaati hain (abhi payable nahi) |
| **5. Payment Received** | Customer kishton mein pay karta hai | % mein record hota hai. Har payment ka reference number unique |
| **6. Payment Completed** | 100% payment | Final buyer details li jaati hain |
| **7. Allotment / Registry** | Dono mein se ek route | Allotment ya Registry |
| **8. Delivered** | Deal poori | Iske baad normal cancellation/Change Plot nahi |

**Beech mein ho sakta hai:** Cancellation/Refund, **Change Plot** (sirf same project mein), Buyback.

**"Sold By" sabse zaroori field hai** — iske sirf 3 option hain: **Member**, **Customer**, **3% Club**. Yahi tay karta hai commission kise milega.

## 7. Commission System — Paanch tarah ki kamaai

### 7.1 Ek nazar mein

| # | Commission | Kise milta hai | Kab | Rate | Kab payable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Direct** | Member jisne plot becha | Har sale par, **unlimited** | **3%** | Customer ka **25%** payment aate hi |
| 2 | **Invite** | Member jisne us seller ko Club mein joda | Har joda hua Member — **zindagi mein ek baar** | 1% / 0.5% / 0.25% | **100%** payment (ya Approved Buyback) |
| 3 | **Royalty** | Member jisne customer ki **pehli purchase** bechi | Us customer ki agli seedhi company purchase par — **ek baar** | 1% / 0.5% / 0.25% | **100%** payment (ya Approved Buyback) |
| 4 | **Loyalty** | Customer | Kisi aur ko plot dilwaaye, ya khud dobara khareede | **1%**, lifetime max **3** | **100%** payment (ya Approved Buyback) |
| 5 | **Buying** | Jo company ke liye property khareedne ka deal laaye | Har acquisition par ek | Case-by-case, **max 5%** | Company ka **100% Payment Given** |

**4% ki limit:** Direct + Invite + Royalty + Loyalty ek sale par **4% se zyada kabhi nahi**. Buying commission is limit ke bahar hai (uski apni 5% limit hai).

### 7.2 Direct Commission — dealer ki roz ki kamaai
- Member ne kisi aur ko plot becha → **3%**, customer ke **25% payment** par.
- Member ne **khud ke liye** khareeda (Primary Customer khud Member) → **3%**, lekin **100% payment** par. Aur us sale par kisi aur ko kuch nahi (na Invite, na Royalty, na Loyalty).
- Do Members ek Direct commission share **nahi** kar sakte — ek sale, ek seller.

### 7.3 Invite — dealer network ka inaam

| Position (jitne log aapne jode) | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 1st, 2nd, 3rd | 1% |
| 4th, 5th, 6th | 0.5% |
| 7th, 8th, 9th | 0.25% |
| 10th aur aage | 0% (dikhta hai, par kuch nahi milta) |

- Sirf **seedha inviter** — upar ki koi chain nahi. **MLM nahi hai.**
- Sirf join karwaane se **kuch nahi** milta. Paisa tab milta hai jab joda hua Member **khud kisi aur ko plot bechta hai** aur woh sale 100% payment tak pahunchti hai.
- Har joda hua Member **ek hi baar** Invite deta hai. Woh 10 plot beche, inviter ko sirf pehli qualifying sale par milta hai.
- Joda hua Member agar khud ke liye khareede, to woh Invite nahi ginta.

**Performance Cycle:** position 1–9 tab tak chalti hain jab tak sab 9 log apni qualifying sale poori na kar lein. Saare 9 successful → **Upgrade Eligible** → Member ki activation anniversary par **naya cycle** (phir se 1% se shuru). Adhoora cycle anniversary par reset **nahi** hota. Ek baar mili position aur rate **kabhi nahi badalte**.

### 7.4 Royalty — "customer aapka hi rahega"
- Member ne kisi customer ki **pehli purchase** becha (Sold By Member) → woh Member us customer ka **Royalty Linked Member** ban jaata hai (pehli purchase 100% payment ya Approved Buyback tak pahunche tab pakka hota hai).
- Wahi customer baad mein **seedha company se** (Sold By 3% Club) dobara khareede → Royalty Linked Member ko **Royalty** milti hai. **Customer ki zindagi mein ek baar.**
- Rate us customer ki position se: pehle 3 customer 1%, agle 3 0.5%, agle 3 0.25%, 10+ 0%. Invite jaisa hi alag cycle.
- Agar repeat purchase **koi doosra Member** bechta hai → us par Royalty nahi, lekin Royalty **bachi rehti hai** agli seedhi company purchase ke liye.
- Customer ki pehli purchase agar company ne ya kisi Customer ne bechi → us customer par **kabhi kisi Member ki Royalty nahi**.
- Sirf Enquiry daalne se Royalty **nahi** milti — sale karni padti hai.

### 7.5 Loyalty — customer ko bhi saath jodna
- Rate **1%**, lifetime maximum **3**.
- Do raaste: (a) customer ne kisi **aur** ko plot dilwaaya (Sold By Customer), ya (b) customer ne **dobara khud** company se seedha khareeda.
- Pehli khud ki purchase par Loyalty **nahi**.
- 3 Loyalty ke baad customer khareed sakta hai, par doosron ko bechne ke liye use **Member banna** padega.
- Customer chahe to pehle hi Member ban sakta hai — bachi hui Loyalty chali jaati hai.

> **Pending change:** CR-004 mein prastaav hai ki dono raaston ki alag-alag 3 Loyalty ho (total 6). **Abhi approve nahi hua hai** — tab tak 3 hi maane jaayenge.

### 7.6 Buying Commission — property dilwaane ka inaam
- Company jab koi property khareedti hai (Buyback ya Purchase for Resale), to jo deal laaye use **case-by-case %** — maximum **5%**, acquisition price par.
- Member, Customer ya **bahar ka broker** bhi le sakta hai. Ek acquisition, ek beneficiary.
- Bechne wala khud apni property wapas bechne ka commission **nahi** le sakta.

### 7.7 Kaunsi sale par kya banta hai

| Sale kisne band ki | Buyer kaun | Kya milta hai | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Koi aur | Direct 3% + Invite (seller ke inviter ko) | 4% tak |
| Member | Khud Member | Sirf Direct 3%, 100% par | 3% |
| Customer | Koi aur | Loyalty 1% us customer ko | 1% |
| 3% Club | Naya buyer (pehli purchase) | **Kuch nahi** | 0% |
| 3% Club | Purana buyer (dobara) | Loyalty 1% buyer ko + Royalty (Royalty Linked Member ko) | 2% tak |

## 8. Commission ka payout — kab aur kaise

**Payout target:** saari shartein poori hone ke **7 working days** ke andar.

Payout se pehle yeh sab clear hona chahiye:

| Shart | Kiske liye |
| --- | --- |
| Milestone payment aa gaya (25% / 100%) | Sab |
| **Aadhaar** record mein | Sab |
| **Bank account verified** | Sab |
| **RERA** — Registered ya Not Applicable (Pending/Expired par rok) | Sirf Member |
| Member **Active** hai, commission hold par nahi | Sirf Member |
| Sale par refund / Change Plot / buyback chal nahi raha | Sab |

Baaki rules:
- **Minimum payout limit nahi** — chhoti rakam bhi milegi.
- Har commission **alag transfer** — ek mein jodkar nahi.
- **PAN** ki kami se payment nahi rukta.
- **TDS / kanooni deductions** Accounts bahar handle karta hai.
- **Paid Early** (shart poori hone se pehle payment) sirf **MD approval** se.

**Recovery:** agar diya hua commission galat nikle (jaise sale cancel ho gayi) → **Recovery Outstanding**. **15 din** mein settle karna hai. Member ne settle nahi kiya → **membership deactivate**. Customer ne nahi kiya → aage ka koi benefit nahi milega.

## 9. Buyback, Resale aur Land

| Kaam | Kya hai | Business value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buyback** | Company apna becha hua plot customer se wapas khareedti hai | Customer ko exit milta hai — bharosa badhta hai. Plot "RESALE" tag ke saath dobara bikta hai |
| **Purchase for Resale** | Bahar se property khareedkar inventory mein daalna | Inventory badhti hai |
| **Land Inquiry** | Zameen maalikon aur dealers se land leads | Naye projects ki pipeline |

- Company **Payment Given** (company ne kitna diya) alag track karti hai — Payment Received se kabhi mix nahi.
- Buyback/Resale approve hone ke liye kam se kam **20% Payment Given** zaroori.
- **Approved Buyback** Invite, Royalty aur Loyalty ke liye 100% payment ka **alternative milestone** hai — Direct ke liye nahi.

## 10. Bharosa aur control — yeh system alag kyun hai

| Control | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| **CRM mein rupaye nahi, sirf %** | Deal value, commission amount CRM mein nahi — sirf %, date, status, reference. Rupaye ka hisaab Accounts ke paas |
| **Maker-checker** | Banaane wala aur approve karne wala alag |
| **Kuch delete nahi hota** | Har galti "correct" hoti hai, purana record history mein rehta hai |
| **Unique payment reference** | Ek reference do baar use nahi ho sakta — double entry band |
| **One person, one record** | Duplicate customer/member merge hote hain, Loyalty dobara gini jaati hai |
| **Double payment protection** | Invite, Royalty, Loyalty ke "slot" ek hi baar use hote hain |
| **Privacy** | Member ko buyer ki identity, doosre Member ka data, full Aadhaar/PAN/bank nahi dikhta |
| **Timezone** | Asia/Kolkata, dates DD/MM/YYYY |

## 11. Member kya kar sakta hai, kya nahi

**Kar sakta hai:**
- Genuine customer aur naye Members laana
- Enquiry daalna, customer ke liye **Hold request** daalna
- Approved inventory aur project material dekhna/share karna
- Apna commission — type, %, milestone, status, hold ka kaaran — portal par dekhna
- Apna network (positions, bands) dekhna

**Nahi kar sakta:**
- Booking, payment, cancellation approve karna
- Customer se **apne account mein paisa lena** ya apni receipt dena
- Bina asli customer ke plot block karna
- Title, return, appreciation, loan, registry ya possession ka **vaada** karna
- Club ko **MLM / chain income / guaranteed return scheme** batana
- Portal login share karna, data copy/export karna

**Membership kisi cheez ki guarantee nahi:** income, leads, sales, territory, exclusivity ya fixed date par commission — kuch guaranteed nahi.

## 12. Indian market mein yeh kyun chalega

1. **Dealer market bikhra hua hai** — lakhon chhote property dealers, koi brand nahi. Club unhe bina fee ke brand aur system deta hai.
2. **Commission ka bharosa sabse badi kami hai** — "likhit 3%, portal par status, 7 din mein payout" seedha is dard ko pakadta hai.
3. **Customer fraud se darta hai** — official payment channel, official receipt aur company process customer ka darr kam karta hai.
4. **WhatsApp-first market** — funnel WhatsApp par hai, jahan dealer roz kaam karta hai.
5. **Tier 2 / Tier 3 shehar** — plotted projects ki demand yahin sabse zyada hai, aur yahin dealer network sabse mazboot hai.
6. **Free membership** — dealer ke liye koi risk nahi, "haan" bolna aasaan.
7. **Rishtey ka paisa** — Royalty aur Loyalty dealer aur customer dono ko lambe samay tak jodte hain; customer bhi promoter ban jaata hai.
8. **RERA compliance** — Member ka RERA status track hota hai, jo aajkal kanooni zaroorat hai.
